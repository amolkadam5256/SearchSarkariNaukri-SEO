## Corrections applied to the supplied draft

- The cited 8 September 2011 O.M. No. 18011/1/2010-Estt.(C) appears in available records as a draft consultation on proposed provisions. It is not used as the operative basis here.
- The 75% leave threshold and extension language are attributed to the 15 December 2021 Lok Sabha answer describing Central Government instructions, not applied to States/PSUs automatically.
- The supplied draft's “deemed confirmation” paragraph is omitted. DoPT's 2019 circular describes written orders and says confirmation is not automatic under the cited Central Services framework.
- Avoid blanket claims that completing a period makes an employee permanent or that termination during probation is always valid/invalid. Individual outcomes depend on governing rules and facts.

## Verification limitations

No Maharashtra-specific, PSU, public-sector bank, or contractual service rule was researched for this general article. Do not add such detailed examples without identifying and checking the actual employer/service rules. This is informational content, not case-specific legal advice.
