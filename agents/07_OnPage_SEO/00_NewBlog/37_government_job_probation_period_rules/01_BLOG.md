Getting selected for a government job is a major achievement, but the appointment may include a probation period before the employee's suitability is formally assessed. During probation, the appointing authority may review performance, conduct, required training, and departmental examinations where the applicable rules prescribe them.

There is no single probation duration or confirmation process for every government job in India. The relevant recruitment rules, service regulations, appointment letter, and later official orders control the terms. This guide explains the general idea and summarizes Central Government instructions without presenting them as universal rules.

## What is a probation period in a government job?

Probation is an initial assessment period after appointment. The Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT) [describes probation in Central services](https://agwb.cag.gov.in/files/ersa/Master_Circular_on_probation_confirmation.pdf) as a way to assess a person's suitability for absorption into the service. It is not simply a waiting period that guarantees a particular employment status after a fixed number of months.

Depending on the post and applicable rules, an employee may be expected to learn departmental procedures, carry out assigned duties, complete induction or role-specific training, pass prescribed departmental examinations, and meet conduct and attendance requirements. Not every service uses the same assessment or requires every item in this list.

## How long is probation?

There is no one duration for every government employee. Check the recruitment rules for the exact post, the appointment letter, service regulations, and any later department or cadre instructions. The Central Government's consolidated instructions include different patterns for different methods of appointment and services; model provisions for certain organized services use two years. That example is not a rule for every job.

UPSC and MPSC are recruiting commissions, not single services with one common probation period. A candidate selected through either commission is appointed to a particular service or post, and the applicable service rules and appointment terms determine probation conditions.

## What can be assessed during probation?

The specific criteria depend on the employer and service. In its [answer to Lok Sabha Unstarred Question No. 2870](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/177/AU2870.pdf?source=pqals), the Central Government identified work performance, mandatory training, and overall conduct as matters primarily considered in confirmation decisions. The DoPT circular also discusses applicable departmental examinations. Assessment may involve reports from supervisors or training institutions where the governing process provides for them.

Examples of possible requirements include:

- Performing the duties of the post to the required standard.
- Completing required induction or technical training.
- Passing a departmental examination where one is prescribed.
- Meeting conduct, attendance, and other service conditions.
- Providing required records or completing administrative formalities.

These are examples, not a universal checklist. The department's actual rules and written instructions are the source to follow.

## Is training compulsory during probation?

Only where the applicable service or recruitment rules, appointment terms, or department instructions require it. Some roles have structured induction or professional training; others may use on-the-job instruction or have no separate formal course. A training example for one department cannot establish the rule for another.

Newly appointed employees should check the joining instructions and ask the relevant office for the training schedule, attendance requirements, examinations, and completion record. Keep copies of certificates or official acknowledgements where issued.

## Can probation be extended?

It may be extended when the governing rules permit it. For Central services, the DoPT Master Circular and a 2021 Lok Sabha answer describe circumstances such as not completing required training or not passing a prescribed departmental examination. The appointing authority may also consider an extension necessary in an individual case under the relevant instructions.

The [Lok Sabha answer](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/177/AU2870.pdf?source=pqals) says that, under the Central Government instructions it describes, when a probationer does not complete 75% of the prescribed duration because of leave, probation may be extended by the length of leave availed. It also states that probation should not exceed double the prescribed period. These are Central Government instructions; check whether they apply to the employee's service and whether later or service-specific instructions govern the case.

If an extension is issued, read the order for its duration, reasons, required actions, and review date. Ask the appointing authority for clarification if a condition or deadline is unclear.

## What is the difference between probation and confirmation?

Probation is the assessment period. Confirmation is a formal employment status or action governed by the applicable service rules and process. Successful completion of a time period and formal confirmation should not be treated as interchangeable without checking those rules.

The Central Government's [2019 Master Circular](https://agwb.cag.gov.in/files/ersa/Master_Circular_on_probation_confirmation.pdf) says successful completion of probation is recorded by a written order. The particular process and effective date can vary with the applicable rules. Do not rely on a general online claim that simply completing one or two years automatically makes every government employee permanent.

| Probation | Confirmation |
|---|---|
| Initial assessment under applicable terms | Formal action/status under the applicable rules |
| May involve training or examinations if prescribed | May depend on satisfactory completion of prescribed conditions and formalities |
| Can be extended where permitted | Should be established from the written order or process that applies |

The word “permanent” can have specific legal and service-rule meanings. Use the terminology in the appointment order and governing rules rather than assuming it follows automatically from confirmation.

## Can a government employee be discharged during probation?

The [Central Government Master Circular](https://agwb.cag.gov.in/files/ersa/Master_Circular_on_probation_confirmation.pdf) recognizes that an appointing authority may extend probation or terminate the services of a temporary employee on probation based on performance evaluation, subject to applicable rules and appointment conditions. Other employers may have different provisions.

The legal effect of a discharge or termination depends on the appointment, governing rules, wording and grounds of the order, and the facts of the case. This general article cannot determine an individual's rights or whether a specific action is lawful. Anyone facing a proposed termination or disputed confirmation should review the actual documents and seek advice from a qualified service-law professional or appropriate employee representative.

## What if a departmental examination is not passed?

First check whether the exam is mandatory for the particular post and what the governing rules say about attempts, passing marks, retakes, and consequences. Depending on those rules, an employee may be required to retake an exam, complete additional training, face an extension, or be subject to another prescribed action. There is no single outcome for all departments.

Do not assume that an examination requirement mentioned in another service's guide applies to your appointment. Ask the department for the current syllabus, schedule, and written rule or order.

## Central Government, State Government, PSU, and contractual appointments

The employer and appointment type matter. The DoPT Master Circular covers Central Government services. State services have their own applicable rules and orders. Public-sector undertakings, public-sector banks, autonomous bodies, and other organizations can have separate regulations or employment conditions. Contractual employment may be governed by a contract rather than the probation framework for regular appointments.

Also distinguish the recruiting commission from the appointing employer. A candidate recruited through UPSC or MPSC should check the rules for the service and cadre they are joining, not assume that the commission itself sets one probation rule for all appointments.

## Probation checklist for a newly appointed employee

- Read the complete appointment letter and joining instructions.
- Obtain or locate the current service and recruitment rules for the post.
- Record the probation start date, expected end date, and any review deadlines.
- Confirm which training and departmental examinations are mandatory.
- Attend required training and keep completion evidence.
- Track attendance, leave, and submissions using official records.
- Ask the department in writing if an instruction or requirement is unclear.
- Keep copies of assessment, extension, and confirmation communications.
- Do not assume confirmation solely from the passage of time; check the applicable process.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a two-year probation period compulsory for every government job?

No. The duration depends on the service, recruitment rules, appointment type, and post. Check the appointment letter and current rules for the specific position.

### Can probation be extended because of leave?

The Central Government instructions described in a 2021 Lok Sabha answer provide for a possible extension in specified cases where leave means the probationer has not completed 75% of the prescribed duration. Check whether those instructions apply to the appointment and whether a service-specific rule controls.

### Does completing probation automatically make an employee permanent?

Do not assume that it does. Confirmation and employment status depend on the applicable service rules and formal process. Check for the required written order or communication.

### Is training required for every government employee on probation?

No single training requirement applies to every post. Training is required when the applicable rules or appointment instructions prescribe it.

### Can a probationer be terminated?

The governing rules and appointment terms may allow discharge or termination during probation. The effect and legality of a particular action depend on its facts and documents; get qualified advice for an individual dispute.

### Do UPSC and MPSC candidates follow the same probation rules?

Not necessarily. The commission conducts recruitment; the appointed service or cadre's rules and the appointment terms determine the probation conditions.

### What should I do if I fail a departmental examination?

Check the specific examination rules for retakes, further training, extension, and other consequences. Ask the appointing department for the current written instructions.

### Where can I confirm my probation conditions?

Start with the appointment letter and current recruitment or service rules. Then check official departmental orders and ask the appointing authority if anything remains unclear.

## What selected candidates should remember

Selection is the start of a new set of responsibilities. Probation may include assessment, training, or examinations, but its rules are specific to the appointment. Read the governing documents, keep written records, and confirm requirements with the appointing department. For questions about an individual employment decision, use the actual order and obtain qualified advice.
