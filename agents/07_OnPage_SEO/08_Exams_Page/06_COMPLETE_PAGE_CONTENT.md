## FAQ

### What are the major government exams in India?

Major government exams in India include UPSC Civil Services, SSC CGL, SSC CHSL, RRB NTPC, RRB Group D, IBPS PO, SBI PO, RBI Grade B, NDA, CDS, CTET and state exams such as MPSC Rajyaseva.

### Which government exams can graduates apply for?

Graduates can commonly apply for exams such as UPSC Civil Services, MPSC Rajyaseva, SSC CGL, IBPS PO, SBI PO, RBI Grade B, CDS and many state-level administrative, banking and technical recruitments. Exact eligibility depends on the official notification.

### Which government exams are available after 12th?

After 12th, candidates may consider SSC CHSL, NDA, some railway posts, some police recruitments and defence recruitments where the official notification permits 12th pass eligibility.

### Which exams are conducted by UPSC?

UPSC conducts exams such as Civil Services Examination, NDA, CDS, CAPF Assistant Commandant, Engineering Services, Combined Medical Services and other central recruitment examinations.
