Use the following status rules:
- Upcoming: the notification is confirmed but applications have not opened.
- Open: applications are currently being accepted according to verified dates and notices.
- Closing soon: the deadline falls within the defined, timezone-aware warning window.
- Closed: the deadline has passed or the authority has closed applications.
- Extended: an official extension has been published and verified.
- Result/selection stage: applications are no longer the primary action and the recruitment has progressed to a later stage.
- Unverified: the record requires review before being presented as an active opportunity.
