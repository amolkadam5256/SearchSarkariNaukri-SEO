## Official-source starting points

Use these as starting points, then verify the responsible authority and exact notice for each actual vacancy:

- SSC: https://ssc.gov.in/
- Railway Recruitment Boards: https://www.rrcb.gov.in/
- India Post vacancies: https://www.indiapost.gov.in/vacancies
- India Post GDS portal: https://www.indiapost.gov.in/gdsonlineengagement
- UPSC: https://upsc.gov.in/
- MPSC: https://mpsc.gov.in/

Add police, defence, banking and state portals only for relevant listings. Label external links clearly. The official notice is the source of truth for eligibility, pay, dates, vacancies and application steps. Do not imply every listing under a broad category accepts Class 12 candidates.
