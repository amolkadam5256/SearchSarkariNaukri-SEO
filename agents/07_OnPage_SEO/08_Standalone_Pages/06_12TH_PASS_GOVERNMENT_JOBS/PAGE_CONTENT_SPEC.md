## Candidate-facing layout rules

- Put the page purpose, active status and search/filter controls near the top.
- Keep job discovery and current vacancies central. Keep long-form career guidance on the separate informational article.
- Clearly label facts that vary by post, state, category or recruitment cycle.
- Keep essential status, deadlines and source links visible without requiring an accordion interaction.
- Use concise copy and descriptive actions; avoid repeating the primary keyword in every heading.
- Add a last-verified label only when a real verification timestamp exists.
