## Section Overview
Clear, factual specifications of today's assessment module. We present actual structural parameters rather than unverified marketing counters.

## Operational Parameters
- **10 Questions** — Standard length for each daily session
- **5 + 5 Balance** — 5 recent current-affairs items paired with 5 core syllabus questions
- **2 Dedicated Paths** — Specialized streams for UPSC Civil Services and MPSC Rajyaseva
- **~10 Minutes** — Typical time needed to complete all questions and review explanations
- **Free Access** — Open practice with free progress tracking via your personal dashboard

## Personal Dashboard Metrics (Logged-in Learners)
When logged into your account, your dashboard tracks:
- Today's completed score and accuracy percentage
- 7-day and 30-day attempt consistency
- Subject-by-subject accuracy breakdown
- Revisit link to review previous incorrect responses
