# 09 — Preparation Strategy

## H2
How to Use the Daily Quiz in Your UPSC or MPSC Study Plan

## Practical Revision Strategy
A 10-question daily quiz is most effective when integrated into a structured preparation workflow rather than attempted haphazardly. Here is a reliable routine:

## Recommended Routine
1. **Pre-Quiz Reading** — Spend 15 to 20 minutes reviewing the morning's national news summaries or official PIB briefs before launching the quiz.
2. **Uninterrupted Attempt** — Solve all 10 questions in a single 10-to-12-minute window. Avoid checking reference books, internet searches, or notes during the attempt.
3. **Analyze Every Missed Question** — Do not just check your final score. Read the complete explanation for every question you answered incorrectly or guessed on.
4. **Maintain an Errata Diary** — Jot down forgotten constitutional articles, government scheme dates, or economic definitions in a dedicated revision notebook.
5. **Conduct Weekly Topic Audits** — At the end of each week, review your dashboard topic breakdown. If multiple errors clustered in Geography or Modern History, allocate your weekend reading blocks to those chapters.
6. **Complement with Full Mock Tests** — Use daily quizzes for consistent recall and factual sharpness, while reserving 100-question full-length mock exams for testing your 2-hour endurance and paper-solving strategy.
