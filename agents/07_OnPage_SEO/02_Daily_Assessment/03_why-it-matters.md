# 03 — Why Daily Assessment Matters

## H2
Why Use a Daily Quiz for UPSC and MPSC Preparation?

## Section Introduction
Preparing for civil services and state administrative exams requires handling an expansive syllabus alongside rapidly evolving current events. Dedicated daily practice helps turn a massive reading load into manageable, daily revision milestones.

## Practical Advantages
- **Consistent revision** — Engaging with 10 questions daily keeps core concepts active in memory without adding unnecessary study fatigue.
- **Active recall** — Solving questions without looking at reference material tests what you genuinely remember versus what only feels familiar while re-reading.
- **Immediate feedback** — Reviewing answer keys and explanations right after submission clarifies facts and concepts while they are still fresh in your mind.
- **Pinpointing study gaps** — Subject-level results highlight the exact topics—such as constitutional articles, economic indicators, or regional schemes—where your preparation needs reinforcement.
- **Connecting news to exam themes** — Regular current-affairs MCQs demonstrate how daily news items translate into actual prelims-style questions.
- **Tracking personal trends** — Reviewing your scores over time gives you an objective picture of your consistency and topic strengths.
