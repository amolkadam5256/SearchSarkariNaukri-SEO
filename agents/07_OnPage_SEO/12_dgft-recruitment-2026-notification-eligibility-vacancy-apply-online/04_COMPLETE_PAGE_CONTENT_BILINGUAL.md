## About DGFT — Directorate General of Foreign Trade

DGFT is the Directorate General of Foreign Trade, an attached office of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. Headquartered in New Delhi, DGFT is responsible for formulating and implementing India's Foreign Trade Policy (FTP), promoting exports, and regulating foreign trade operations.

Job applicants should distinguish the official government department from independent websites that aggregate and republish recruitment notices. The official DGFT website is **[https://www.dgft.gov.in/](https://www.dgft.gov.in/)**.
