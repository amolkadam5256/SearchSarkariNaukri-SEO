## 3. Editorial Linking Rules & Anti-Anchor-Spam Policy

1. **Natural Anchor Text:** Use contextual, human-readable anchor phrases (e.g. `DGFT recruitment verification guide` or `previous DGFT 2026 recruitment archive`). Avoid repeated exact-match anchors like `DGFT Recruitment 2026` across all internal blog links.
2. **Never Mislead Readers:** Never feature the archived DGFT listing under headings like "Latest Job Openings Today" or "Apply for Government Jobs This Month".
3. **Outbound Link Hygiene:** Any external link pointing to `https://www.dgft.gov.in/` from blogs must include `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
