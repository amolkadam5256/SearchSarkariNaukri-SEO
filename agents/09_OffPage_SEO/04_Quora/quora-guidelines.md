## ✅ Question Format (Daily — Ask 2–10)

### Question Topics (Rotate)
- UPSC/MPSC exam preparation tips
- Sarkari Naukri eligibility & application process
- Government job categories (Railway, Banking, State PSC, SSC)
- Admit card & result queries
- Maharashtra government jobs (NMK/Talathi/Police)
- Salary & career growth in government jobs

### Question Writing Rules
1. Make the question **specific and searchable** (mirrors real user queries).
2. Use keywords naturally.
3. Add context in the "Question Details" section.
4. Examples:
   - `What is the best strategy to crack UPSC Prelims in 6 months?`
   - `How can I find the latest government job vacancies in Maharashtra?`
   - `What documents are required for MPSC application 2026?`
   - `What is the salary of a Talathi in Maharashtra after the 7th Pay Commission?`
   - `How do I check my RRB exam result online?`
