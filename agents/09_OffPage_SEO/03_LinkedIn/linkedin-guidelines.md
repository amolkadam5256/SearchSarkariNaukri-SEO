## ✅ Daily LinkedIn Post Format

### Post Types (Rotate Daily)
1. **Job Alert Post** — Announce new/latest Sarkari Naukri openings.
2. **Blog Promo Post** — Share the day's Medium or Blogger post.
3. **Exam Tips / Study Tips** — Quick actionable tips for aspirants.
4. **Result / Admit Card Alert** — Notify about exam results or admit cards.
5. **Motivational / Engagement Post** — Polls, questions, aspirant stories.
6. **Infographic / Carousel** — Visual summary of government job categories.

---


### Link Rules
- Every post must include **at least 1 link** to searchsarkarinaukri.com.
- Match the link to the post topic (e.g., job alert → job listing page, exam tip → blog page).
- Use **UTM parameters** for tracking (optional but recommended):
  `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=daily-post`

## 📌 LinkedIn Page Management Best Practices

1. **Respond to comments** within 24 hours to boost engagement.
2. **Follow relevant pages** (UPSC, MPSC, SSC, banking exam pages) to build network.
3. **Tag relevant people/organizations** when appropriate.
4. **Repost** high-performing content from the SSN website.
5. **Monitor page analytics** weekly — track impressions, clicks, follower growth.
6. **Engage with comments** on other relevant posts to grow organic reach.
7. Share every Medium and Blogger post on LinkedIn the same day.
