## ✅ Approved Post Format (Follow Every Time)

### 1. 🏷️ Title (H1 — SEO-Focused)
- Include the **primary keyword** naturally at the start.
- Keep it under **60 characters** for SERPs.
- Make it click-worthy and clear.
- **Example:** `How to Measure Your UPSC Preparation Progress: A Practical Tracker`

### 2. 📝 Subtitle (Kicker / Sub-headline)
- Expand on the title promise.
- Include a **secondary keyword**.
- 1–2 sentences max.

### 3. 🖼️ Featured Image
- Format: **16:9 ratio** (recommended: 1200×675 px or 1280×720 px)
- High-quality, topic-relevant creative.
- Add descriptive **alt text** with primary keyword.

### 4. 📖 Body Content
- Minimum **800–1200 words**.
- Use H2 / H3 subheadings for structure.
- Include **10–20 contextual internal links** to searchsarkarinaukri.com pages (from sitemaps below).
- Write in simple, clear English targeted at government job aspirants.
- Include relevant statistics, examples, or step-by-step guides.

### 6. 🏷️ Topics / Tags (Medium)
- Add **5–8 relevant topics/tags**.
- Examples: `UPSC`, `Sarkari Naukri`, `Government Jobs`, `MPSC`, `Job Preparation`, `Current Affairs`, `India`.

### 7. ❓ FAQ Section (Minimum 5 Q&As)
- Place FAQs before the CTA.
- Base questions on real search queries (use Google's "People Also Ask" as reference).
- Format:
  ```
  **Q: [Question here?]**
  A: [Short, helpful answer. Include 1 internal link where relevant.]
  ```

### 8. 📣 CTA (Call to Action)
- Always end with a strong CTA.
- Direct readers to searchsarkarinaukri.com.
- **Template:**
  > Ready to find your next government job opportunity? Visit **[Search Sarkari Naukri](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com)** — India's trusted portal for the latest Sarkari Naukri, exam notifications, admit cards, and results. **Bookmark us today!**

### 9. 🔗 Canonical URL
- In the Medium post footer / "Originally published at" section, always add:
  - **Canonical / Original URL:** Link to the relevant page on `searchsarkarinaukri.com`
  - **Example:** `Originally published at https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/blog/[slug]`

## 📌 SEO Best Practices for Medium

1. **Publish consistently** — daily posts signal activity to Google.
2. **Avoid duplicate content** — each Medium post must be unique from Blogger.
3. **Use "Originally published at"** to set canonical correctly (avoids duplicate content penalty).
4. **Engage in comments** — respond to readers to boost engagement signals.
5. **Share each post** on LinkedIn and Quora Space after publishing.
