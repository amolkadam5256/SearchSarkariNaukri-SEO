---

## ✅ Approved Post Format (Follow Every Time)

### 1. 🏷️ Title
- **SEO-focused**, primary keyword at the start.
- Under 60 characters for SERP display.
- Should complement (not duplicate) the Medium post title.

### 2. 📝 Search Description (Blogger Meta Description)
- Fill the **"Search Description"** field in Blogger Settings.
- 150–160 characters.
- Include primary keyword + secondary keyword + value proposition.
- **Example:** `Learn how to track your UPSC preparation progress with a free practical tracker. Daily tips, resources & government job updates at SearchSarkariNaukri.`

### 3. 🔗 Permalink (Custom URL Slug)
- Always set a **custom permalink** in Blogger (do not use auto-generated).
- Format: `/YYYY/MM/keyword-rich-slug.html`
- **Example:** `/2026/09/how-to-measure-upsc-preparation-progress.html`
- Keep slug short, keyword-rich, and hyphen-separated.

### 4. 🖼️ Featured Image
- Format: **16:9 ratio** (1200×675 px or 1280×720 px).
- Use high-quality, topic-relevant creative.
- Add descriptive **alt text** with primary keyword.

### 5. 📖 Body Content
- Minimum **800–1200 words**.
- Use H2 / H3 subheadings for structure.
- Write for government job aspirants in India.
- Must be **unique from the Medium version** (rewrite, rephrase, or expand).

### 7. 🏷️ Labels (Blogger Tags)
- Add **5–10 relevant labels**.
- Examples: `UPSC`, `MPSC`, `Sarkari Naukri`, `Government Jobs`, `Exam Preparation`, `Current Affairs`, `Maharashtra Jobs`, `Central Government Jobs`.

### 8. ❓ FAQ Section (Minimum 5 Q&As)
- Place FAQs before the CTA.
- Format:
  ```

  A: [Short, helpful answer. Include 1 internal SSN link where relevant.]
  ```

## 📌 Blogger SEO Best Practices

1. **Custom permalink** is mandatory — Blogger auto-slugs are SEO-unfriendly.
2. **Search Description** must be filled — it becomes the meta description.
3. Always add **labels** — they act as category pages in Blogger.
4. **Submit URL** to Google Search Console after each post.
5. **Share post** on LinkedIn and Quora immediately after publishing.
6. Ensure content is **substantially different** from Medium version to avoid duplicate content.
