## How to Check / Download the Admit Card

1. Open the official MPSC website.
2. Look for the Rajyaseva / State Services examination section.
3. Open the admit-card or hall-ticket notice when it is published.
4. Enter the credentials requested by MPSC.
5. Submit the details and open the admit card.
6. Check your name, roll/application number, exam date and centre details.
7. Download and print the admit card if the official instructions require a printed copy.

Once the hall ticket is released, carefully verify the candidate name, roll/application number, examination date, reporting time, examination centre and any instructions printed by the authority. If any detail appears incorrect, follow the correction/helpdesk process stated in the official notice.

- Try again after a short interval if the portal is busy.
- Try a current browser or clear cached site data.
- Read the official notice/helpdesk instructions.
- Do not use an unofficial download link for the final admit card.
