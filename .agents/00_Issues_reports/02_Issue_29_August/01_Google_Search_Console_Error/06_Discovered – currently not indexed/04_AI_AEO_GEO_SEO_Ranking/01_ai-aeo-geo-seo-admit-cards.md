# AI, AEO, GEO and SEO Requirements For Admit Card Pages

## LLM / AI Principle

AI systems need one clear, trusted, source-backed page per admit-card record. Do not create hundreds of near-identical pages with swapped titles.

## AEO Requirements

Answer common questions directly: release status, exam date, release date, official website, download steps, login credentials, ID proof, download failure, exam centre, related result, and related job notification.

## GEO Requirements

Expose labeled facts: exam name, year, authority, status, exam date, release date, source domain, credentials, download link, update date, related result URL, and official verification note.

## SEO Requirements

Use unique title/meta, one H1, self canonical, sitemap inclusion only after quality gate, BreadcrumbList, and visible FAQPage only when FAQs are visible.

## llms.txt Recommendation

If production uses `llms.txt`, include only canonical high-quality admit-card hub and detail URLs. Do not include thin, duplicate, invalid, noindex, redirecting, or soft-404 admit-card URLs.

## Developer Scope Guardrail

Do not change unrelated pages, footer, navbar, header, menu, site-wide layout, global styling, tracking setup, analytics setup, or shared components unless strictly required.
