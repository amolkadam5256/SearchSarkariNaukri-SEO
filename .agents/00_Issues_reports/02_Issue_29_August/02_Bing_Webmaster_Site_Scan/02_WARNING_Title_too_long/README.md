## Title Fix Rules

- Keep title under 70 characters.
- Put the primary entity first.
- Include year only where useful.
- Avoid repeating organization/post words.
- Avoid raw database text, malformed separators, and duplicate phrases.
- Make every title unique.

## Title Templates

- Job page: {Organization} {Post} Recruitment {Year}
- Result page: {Exam/Post} Result {Year}
- Admit card page: {Exam Name} Admit Card {Year}
- District/category page: {Category} Jobs in {Location} {Year}
- Department page: {Department} Recruitment {Year}

## SEO/AEO/GEO Notes

A short title alone is not enough. The page must also have matching H1, useful content, canonical consistency, schema consistency, sitemap eligibility, and internal links.

## Developer Scope Guardrail

Do not change unrelated pages, footer, navbar, header, menu, global layout, global styles, analytics, tracking, or shared site-wide components unless the change is strictly required to fix this specific Bing Webmaster Site Scan issue.
