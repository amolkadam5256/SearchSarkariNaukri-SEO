# Keyword-Research-Strategy

## Overview

This document outlines the comprehensive keyword research strategy for SearchSarkariNaukri.com, a leading portal for government job opportunities in India.

## Purpose

To identify, categorize, and prioritize keywords that drive organic traffic, enhance visibility for job seekers, and align with the platform's mission of providing accurate government recruitment information.

## Research Goal

- Increase organic visibility for government job searches
- Capture traffic for high-intent terms like "sarkari naukri 2026"
- Localize content for state-specific job seekers
- Build long-tail content for informational queries

## Data Sources

- SearchSarkariNaukri-Keyword-List-899.xlsx (Primary source)
- Google Search Console
- Competitor analysis
- User search behavior patterns

## Keyword Categories

| Category | Count | Description |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| Core Head Terms | ~200 | Brand/navigational terms like "sarkari naukri" |
| Department/Exam-wise | ~150 | Exam-specific terms like "SSC CGL", "UPSC" |
| District-wise | ~100 | Location-specific district-level searches |
| Informational/Long-tail | ~300 | Detailed queries about jobs, exams, results |
| Qualification-wise | ~80 | Education-level specific searches |
| State-wise | ~500 | State-specific government job searches |

## Search Intent

| Intent Type | Percentage | Strategy |
|-------------|------------|----------|
| Navigational/Commercial | 60% | Optimize homepage and brand terms |
| Informational | 30% | Create detailed guides and articles |
| Transactional | 10% | Job alert subscription pages |

## Research Process

1. Collect seed keywords from Excel dataset
2. Analyze search intent and categorize
3. Prioritize by search volume and competition
4. Map to content templates
5. Validate with competitor analysis

## Best Practices

- Focus on P0 keywords first (highest priority)
- Maintain consistent naming across state hubs
- Update keyword list quarterly
- Track performance in GA4
