## 4. Sample Practice Questions with Explanations

Here is a preview of the question caliber and explanation depth featured in our daily assessments:

### Question 1: Indian Polity (UPSC / MPSC Common Core)
**Topic:** Election Commission of India & Constitutional Mandates  
**Difficulty Level:** Moderate  
**Exam Relevance:** UPSC CSE Prelims GS-I, MPSC Rajyaseva GS-II  

**Q:** Consider the following statements regarding the Election Commission of India (ECI):  
1. The superintendence, direction, and control of elections to Parliament and State Legislatures are vested in the ECI under Article 324 of the Constitution.  
2. The conditions of service and tenure of office of the Election Commissioners are determined by the Chief Election Commissioner.  
3. The Election Commission conducts elections to the offices of the President and Vice-President of India, as well as local Panchayats and Municipalities.  

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?  
* (A) 1 only  
* (B) 1 and 2 only  
* (C) 2 and 3 only  
* (D) 1, 2, and 3  

**Correct Answer:** **(A) 1 only**  

> [!TIP]
> **Detailed Explanation:**  
> * **Statement 1 is correct:** Article 324(1) of the Constitution vests the superintendence, direction, and control of the preparation of electoral rolls and the conduct of elections to Parliament and to the Legislature of every State in the Election Commission.  
> * **Statement 2 is incorrect:** Under Article 324(5), the conditions of service and tenure of office of the Election Commissioners and Regional Commissioners are determined by the **President of India**, subject to any law made by Parliament (not by the CEC).  
> * **Statement 3 is incorrect:** While ECI conducts elections to Parliament, State Legislatures, and the offices of the President and Vice-President, elections to local Panchayats and Municipalities are conducted exclusively by the respective **State Election Commissions (SECs)** under Articles 243K and 243ZA.  
> **Official Citation:** [Constitution of India – Article 324 (legislative.gov.in)](https://legislative.gov.in/constitution-of-india/) and [Election Commission of India (eci.gov.in)](https://www.eci.gov.in/).

### Question 2: Maharashtra Administration & Public Policy (MPSC Focus)
**Topic:** Good Governance & Public Service Guarantee  
**Difficulty Level:** High  
**Exam Relevance:** MPSC Rajyaseva GS-II, MPSC Combined Group B  

**Q:** Under the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015, which of the following statements is accurate regarding the appellate mechanism?  
1. A citizen aggrieved by non-delivery of notified service within the stipulated time can file a First Appeal to the designated First Appellate Authority.  
2. The Maharashtra State Right to Service Commission is headed by a Chief Commissioner appointed by the Governor.  
3. Failure of the public servant to provide notified service without reasonable cause can attract a monetary penalty up to ₹5,000 deducted from their salary.  

Select the correct option using the codes below:  
* (A) 1 and 2 only  
* (B) 2 and 3 only  
* (C) 1, 2, and 3  
* (D) 1 only  

**Correct Answer:** **(C) 1, 2, and 3**  

> [!TIP]
> **Detailed Explanation:**  
> Enacted to promote transparency and accountability in public administration, the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act provides an enforceable statutory framework. A citizen has the legal right to obtain designated public services within specified timelines. If services are delayed or rejected unjustly, the citizen can appeal to the First Appellate Authority, followed by a Second Appellate Authority and the State Right to Service Commission. The Commission possesses powers to penalize defaulting officers between ₹500 and ₹5,000 per default.  
> **Official Citation:** [Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act (maharashtra.gov.in)](https://www.maharashtra.gov.in/).
