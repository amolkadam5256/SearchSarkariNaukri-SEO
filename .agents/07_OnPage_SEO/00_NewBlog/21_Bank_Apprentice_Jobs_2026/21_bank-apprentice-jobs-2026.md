# Bank Apprentice Jobs 2026: Graduate Apprentice Apply Guide

Public-sector banks periodically run **graduate apprentice** programmes — training slots for degree holders, often state-wise merit lists, not the same as direct PO/Clerk selection. These drives generate high search volume because the qualification bar is clear and the application window is short.

**Direct answer:** **Bank apprentice jobs 2026** are usually **graduate apprenticeship** advertisements published by individual banks (for example public-sector banks on their official careers pages). Apply only on the bank’s official recruitment URL in the PDF; apprenticeship is training for a fixed period — read whether it leads to permanent absorption (most notifications explain this explicitly).

## Quick Facts

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Typical qualification | Graduate (degree) — discipline rules in PDF |
| Recruiters | Public-sector banks (e.g. nationalised banks’ official sites) |
| Not the same as | IBPS PO/Clerk direct recruitment (separate exams) |
| Official pattern | Bank careers portal + notification PDF |
| SSN | Lists apprenticeships on [jobs](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/jobs) when verified |

## Apprentice vs PO/Clerk

| | Graduate apprentice | IBPS PO/Clerk |
|---|---------------------|---------------|
| Purpose | Training / apprenticeship | Direct bank officer/clerk selection |
| Apply via | Bank’s own notification | IBPS / bank exam portal |
| Prep hub | [Banking exams](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/exams/sbi-po-clerk) | Same + IBPS official site |

## Who can apply?

- Degree completion dates as in notification (often “graduated between X and Y”).
- Age min/max on cutoff date.
- State-wise quotas may apply — merit often state-wise.
- Local language requirement may apply for some banks.

Check each listing on Search Sarkari Naukri and open the **official PDF**.

## How to apply (steps)

1. Find apprenticeship notification on official bank website (careers section).
2. Download PDF — note state-wise vacancies and dates.
3. Register on the **exact** apply link in PDF.
4. Upload marksheets, photo, signature.
5. Pay fee if required; submit; save proof.

Also read: [government jobs guide 2026](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/guide/government-jobs-2026).

## Stipend and duration

Stipend and training duration are defined in the **apprenticeship contract** in the notification — do not share Aadhaar/bank details with unofficial “registration agents.”

## Avoid fake bank job scams

- No legitimate bank asks for cash to “guarantee” apprenticeship.
- URL should match the bank’s domain, not a random form host.
- Cross-check on [editorial policy](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/editorial-policy).

## FAQ

**Is bank apprentice a permanent job?** Usually apprenticeship for a fixed term; absorption rules are in the notification.

**Can final-year students apply?** Only if notification allows; most require completed degree by a cutoff date.

**IBPS apprentice?** IBPS runs separate exams — apprentice ads are often bank-specific.
