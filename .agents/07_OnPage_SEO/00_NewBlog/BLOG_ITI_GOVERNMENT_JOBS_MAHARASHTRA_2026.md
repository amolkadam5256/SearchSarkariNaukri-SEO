# ITI Government Jobs in Maharashtra 2026: Latest Recruitment, Trades & Eligibility

If you have completed ITI and are looking for a government job in Maharashtra, you do not have to wait for one particular recruitment exam.

Technical vacancies come from different organisations throughout the year. Railways, electricity companies, metro projects, government factories, public-sector organisations and other departments may recruit candidates with ITI qualifications.

The important part is knowing which recruitment matches your ITI trade.

An Electrician should not search for the same vacancies as a Fitter. A Welder may have different opportunities from an Electronics Mechanic. Some notifications accept several trades, while others are limited to one or two specific trades.

This is why ITI candidates should look beyond the general term "government job" and search according to their trade, location and qualification.

## What are ITI government jobs in Maharashtra?

ITI government jobs are technical and support positions for candidates who have completed an Industrial Training Institute course in a recognised trade.

Depending on the recruitment, ITI candidates may find opportunities in:

* Electricity and power organisations
* Railways
* Metro rail
* Government workshops
* Government factories
* Public-sector organisations
* Technical departments
* Transport organisations
* Defence-related establishments
* Apprenticeship programmes
* Maintenance and technician roles

The exact qualification depends on the post.

Some vacancies may require only 10th plus ITI, while others may ask for apprenticeship experience, a specific trade, a particular percentage or an additional qualification.

## Which ITI trades can apply for government jobs?

There is no single list that applies to every recruitment.

Common ITI trades that can appear in government and public-sector recruitment include:

### Electrician
Electrician candidates can find opportunities in electricity organisations, railways, metro projects, workshops and other technical establishments.

### Fitter
Fitter is another widely used technical trade. Recruitment can come from railways, manufacturing units, workshops and public-sector organisations.

### Welder
Welder candidates may find vacancies in workshops, manufacturing establishments, railway organisations and government technical units.

### Electronics Mechanic
Electronics Mechanic candidates can look for technical maintenance, railway, metro and electronics-related opportunities.

### COPA
COPA candidates may find computer-related support positions and apprenticeship opportunities, depending on the recruitment.

Other recognised ITI trades can also be eligible for recruitment. The important thing is to check the trade mentioned in the official notification rather than assuming that every ITI candidate is eligible.

## What is the difference between an ITI job and an ITI apprenticeship?

This is something every ITI candidate should understand before applying.

An apprenticeship is primarily a training opportunity. It can provide practical workplace experience and help a candidate understand how their trade is used in an actual organisation.

A regular government recruitment post is different.

It may offer a permanent or other defined employment position depending on the service rules and recruitment notification.

Therefore, do not assume:

Apprenticeship = permanent government job

It does not.

Always check whether the notification is for:

* Apprentice
* Technician
* Assistant
* Skilled worker
* Trade apprentice
* Contractual employee
* Permanent recruitment

The employment conditions can be completely different.

## Can I get a government job after ITI without a diploma?

Yes, some government and public-sector technical vacancies accept ITI qualifications without requiring a diploma.

But this depends on the post.

For example, one notification may accept:

10th + ITI

while another may require:

10th + ITI + apprenticeship

and another technical post may require:

Diploma in Engineering

The qualification written in the recruitment notification is what matters.

Do not apply simply because a website title says "ITI jobs".

Read the detailed eligibility.

## What should you check before applying?

Before starting an application, check these points.

### 1. ITI trade
Does your exact trade appear in the notification?

### 2. Educational qualification
Does the post require only ITI, or does it also require another qualification?

### 3. Age limit
Check the age calculation date mentioned in the notification.

### 4. Experience
Some technical posts may require previous experience or apprenticeship training.

### 5. Location
Find out where the selected candidate will be posted.

### 6. Selection process
Check whether selection is based on:

* Merit
* Written examination
* Trade test
* Skill test
* Interview
* Document verification
* Medical examination

### 7. Application method
Some recruitments are completely online. Others may require offline applications or applications through a specific portal.

## A common mistake ITI candidates make

Many candidates search for a job only after seeing the recruitment notification.

By that time, they may not have their documents ready, their certificate may need updating, or they may discover that their trade is not eligible.

A better approach is to prepare before recruitment opens.

Keep your ITI certificate, marksheets, identity documents and other commonly required certificates ready.

Then, when a suitable recruitment appears, you can concentrate on checking eligibility and submitting the application correctly.

## What about MPSC or other graduate-level jobs?

ITI candidates should understand that not every Maharashtra government recruitment is meant for ITI qualification.

For example, MPSC Rajyaseva recruitment generally belongs to a different qualification and examination category.

So if you are an ITI candidate, do not mix every Maharashtra government recruitment into your preparation plan.

Start with vacancies that actually accept your qualification.

Once you become eligible for higher qualifications in the future, you can expand your options.

## How to avoid fake recruitment information

Government-job candidates are frequent targets of misleading recruitment messages.

Before applying, verify:

* Recruiting organisation
* Official notification
* Application website
* Last date
* Eligibility
* Vacancy details
* Application fee

Do not send money to an individual who promises selection.

A genuine recruitment notification should provide the official application process and the terms of recruitment.

## Final advice for ITI candidates in Maharashtra

An ITI certificate can lead to several different career paths, but there is no single recruitment route for every trade.

The most practical approach is to identify your trade first, decide which locations you are willing to work in and then follow the organisations that regularly recruit for your skill.

For example, an Electrician candidate may focus on electricity companies and technical establishments. A Fitter may find more suitable opportunities in railways, workshops or manufacturing organisations. A candidate from Nagpur may give priority to recruitment in Nagpur before looking elsewhere.

The goal is not to apply for every ITI vacancy.

The goal is to find the vacancies for which your trade, qualification, age and location actually match.

For the latest ITI government recruitment in Maharashtra, check the notification carefully and verify all important details from the official recruiting organisation before applying.
