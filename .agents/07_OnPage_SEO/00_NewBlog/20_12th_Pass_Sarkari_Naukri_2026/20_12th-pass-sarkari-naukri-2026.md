# 12th Pass Sarkari Naukri 2026: Jobs, Eligibility & How to Apply

If you have completed Class 12, you already qualify for a large slice of central and state **government jobs** — clerical posts, constable-level police recruitment, railway NTPC (12th level where notified), SSC CHSL, and many state board vacancies. The hard part is not “whether 12th pass jobs exist”; it is picking notifications you actually qualify for and applying on the **official** portal before the last date.

**Direct answer:** **12th pass Sarkari Naukri** means government recruitment where the minimum qualification is Higher Secondary (10+2) or equivalent. Open the official notification, confirm age and domicile, then apply on the recruiting authority’s website — not on informal Telegram links.

## Which jobs are common after 12th?

### SSC CHSL
Central ministries — LDC, JSA, DEO-type posts (names per notification). Apply guide: [SSC CHSL 2026 apply online](/blogs/ssc-chsl-2026-apply-online-guide).

### Railway
RRB recruitments specify qualification post-wise. Graduate and 12th streams are **different** CENs — read carefully.

### Police & defence
Many constable and soldier entries accept 12th pass; physical tests are standard.

### Banking
Clerk and apprentice posts often need graduation — do not assume 12th eligibility without reading the bank notice. See [bank apprentice guide](/blogs/bank-apprentice-jobs-2026).

## 12th pass jobs by state

State PSCs and departments publish separate calendars. Maharashtra candidates: [Maharashtra government jobs 2026](/blogs/maharashtra-government-jobs-2026), [districts](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/districts), [Pune jobs](/blogs/government-jobs-in-pune-2026), [Nagpur bharti](/blogs/nagpur-government-bharti-2026).

## Eligibility checklist

- 12th pass certificate from recognised board
- Age on closing date (use [age calculator](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/age-calculator))
- Category certificate if claiming relaxation
- Language / domicile if required
- Physical standards for police/defence

## Salary after 12th government jobs

Pay depends on **pay level** in the 7th CPC matrix and post type — not on “12th pass” alone. The notification states pay band/level. Do not trust viral “₹X salary” posts without PDF proof.

## Documents usually required

10th certificate (DOB), 12th marksheet, photo, signature, ID, category/PwBD certificates, domicile if required.

## 12th pass government jobs by department

### Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
SSC CHSL is the flagship 10+2 central exam. SSC also notifies other exams where 12th may qualify — each advertisement is separate. Hub: [SSC exams](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/exams) · Apply walkthrough: [SSC CHSL 2026 apply online](/blogs/ssc-chsl-2026-apply-online-guide).

### Railway (RRB)
Railway notices are split by qualification level. Confirm whether your CEN is 12th or graduate before applying. Guide: [RRB NTPC graduate recruitment 2026](/blogs/rrb-ntpc-graduate-recruitment-2026) · [RRB NTPC exam page](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/exams/rrb-ntpc).

### Police
State police constable ads usually require 12th pass plus physical standards. Maharashtra: [Police Bharti exam hub](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/exams/maharashtra-police-bharti).

### Post office
India Post cycles (GDS, MTS, etc.) define qualification in each PDF — some are 10th, some 12th.

5. Upload documents; pay fee; submit; save confirmation.

## How to verify before you apply

- Advertisement number and recruiting authority name match the PDF.
- Apply URL belongs to `.gov.in`, `.nic.in`, or the official bank/PSC domain.
- Last date and time match the notification (check corrigendum).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which government jobs are available after 12th?
SSC CHSL, many state police constable posts, some railway CENs, state clerical posts, and other notifications that explicitly require 10+2 — each notice lists posts separately.

### Can I get a government job after 12th only?
Yes. Many permanent and contractual posts exist at 12th level; higher posts need graduation or professional degrees.

### Which is the best Sarkari exam after 12th?
There is no single best exam — CHSL suits central office posts; state police suits physical candidates; railway suits those meeting specific CEN rules.

### Can 12th pass apply for railway jobs?
Yes when the active RRB notification lists 12th eligibility for those posts — graduate NTPC is a different qualification band.

### Are there 12th pass jobs in Maharashtra?
Yes — state departments, ZP, police, and central posts with state quotas. See [Maharashtra government jobs](/blogs/maharashtra-government-jobs-2026) and [districts](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/districts).

### What documents are required?
10th certificate, 12th marksheet, photo, signature, ID, and category/PwBD/domicile certificates if claimed.

### What is the age limit?
Defined per notification — see [age limit for government jobs](/blogs/age-limit-government-jobs-2026).

### Is there an application fee?
Most central and state exams charge a fee with exemptions for some categories — fee table is in the PDF.

### Where can I find latest 12th pass vacancies?
[Jobs](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/jobs), [job updates](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/job-updates), and official PSC/SSC/RRB sites.

### Is Search Sarkari Naukri official?
No. We are an independent information portal. Apply only through the official recruitment notification.
