# Maharashtra Government Jobs 2026: Latest Vacancies & Recruitment

Candidates looking for a stable career in the state public sector have access to thousands of Maharashtra government jobs annually. Recruitment is conducted by distinct departments—such as the Maharashtra Public Service Commission (MPSC), State Police, Revenue Department, and local Zilla Parishads (ZP). Because requirements change based on the specific hiring authority, applicants must always verify eligibility and deadlines through the official recruitment notification.

**Direct Answer: What are Maharashtra government jobs?**
These are public sector employment opportunities offered by the Government of Maharashtra and its subordinate departments, ranging from Class IV (Peon, MTS) to Class I (Gazetted Officers via MPSC). 

To ensure you don’t miss out, you can track the [latest job updates](/job-updates) and verify current active notifications directly on official state portals.

## Maharashtra Government Jobs by Qualification

The state government recruits candidates at almost every educational level. However, simply having a degree or certificate does not guarantee eligibility; you must meet the exact criteria defined by the hiring department.

### 10th Pass Government Jobs in Maharashtra
Candidates who have passed Class 10 (SSC) can apply for entry-level posts such as Peon, Watchman, Sweeper, and Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS). Some Zilla Parishad roles, like certain health worker assistants or Anganwadi workers, also recruit based on 10th-standard marks.

### 12th Pass Government Jobs in Maharashtra
A Class 12 (HSC) certificate opens doors to clerical roles, stenography, and crucially, the [Maharashtra Police Bharti](/exams/maharashtra-police-bharti). Typing speed certificates (English/Marathi) are often mandatory for clerical vacancies.

### ITI Government Jobs in Maharashtra
Departments like Mahatransco, Mahagenco, MSEDCL, and the state transport corporation (MSRTC) frequently hire candidates possessing an Industrial Training Institute (ITI) certificate for technical roles like Lineman, Electrician, and Fitter.

### Diploma Government Jobs in Maharashtra
A 3-year polytechnic diploma is usually the baseline requirement for Junior Engineer (JE) roles across the Public Works Department (PWD), Water Resources Department (WRD), and municipal corporations.

### Graduate Government Jobs in Maharashtra
Graduates can sit for MPSC examinations, apply for Talathi recruitment, or compete for higher-level administrative positions in Zilla Parishads and municipal bodies. Specialized degrees (like B.E., B.Sc. Agri, or B.Pharm) are required for technical officer roles. 

## Maharashtra Government Jobs by Department

State recruitment is highly decentralized. Here is a breakdown of major departments that frequently release vacancies.

### MPSC Government Jobs
The Maharashtra Public Service Commission is the premier recruiting body for the state. It conducts the State Services Examination (Rajyaseva), Subordinate Services (Group B), and Group C examinations. MPSC recruits Deputy Collectors, DSPs, Tehsildars, and Police Sub-Inspectors (PSI). For complete details, syllabus, and updates, check our dedicated [MPSC Rajyaseva](/exams/mpsc-rajyaseva) page. Official MPSC information must always be verified at `mpsc.gov.in`.

### Maharashtra Police Bharti
The state police force conducts massive recruitment drives for Constables, Armed Police (SRPF), and Drivers. Eligibility heavily depends on physical fitness, chest/height measurements, and endurance tests, followed by a written exam. Vacancies are released district-wise. 

### Talathi Recruitment
Managed by the Revenue and Forest Department, Talathi jobs are highly sought after. A Talathi is a village accountant responsible for maintaining crop and land records. Candidates should check the qualification (usually graduation), age limits, district-wise vacancy distribution, application dates, and the specific selection process outlined in the official notification before applying.

### ZP Government Jobs in Maharashtra
Each district's Zilla Parishad conducts its own recruitment for rural administration, health, and education sectors. Roles include Gram Sevak, Arogya Sevak (Health Worker), Pharmacist, and primary school teachers. Candidates must look for notifications from the specific ZP they wish to work for.

### Municipal/Local Government
Municipal Corporations (like BMC, PMC, NMC) hire independently for fire brigade staff, ward boys, clerical assistants, and municipal engineers.

### Health, Education, and Agriculture
The Public Health Department (Arogya Vibhag) recruits nurses and technicians. The Education Department manages teacher recruitment (often via the TET/TAIT exams), while the Agriculture Department hires Krishi Sevaks and technical officers.

## Government Jobs in Maharashtra by District

State recruitment is frequently distributed by region. Applicants must check domicile requirements, as some district-level jobs prioritize local residents. You can filter current active recruitment by checking our [districts](/districts) page.

* **Pune:** High concentration of IT, education, and municipal jobs (PMC/PCMC).
* **Mumbai:** BMC, Mantralaya, and state headquarters recruitment. 
* **Nagpur:** Nagpur Municipal Corporation (NMC), ZP, and regional state offices.
* **Nashik:** Nashik ZP, municipal, and regional revenue recruitment.
* **Thane:** Thane Municipal Corporation (TMC) and highly populated ZP zones.
* **Aurangabad / Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar:** Regional administrative headquarters for Marathwada.
* **Kolhapur, Solapur, Satara, Sangli:** Strong ZP and agricultural recruitment zones.
* **Ahmednagar / Ahilyanagar:** The largest district in Maharashtra, featuring extensive rural development recruitment.

## How to Check Eligibility for a Maharashtra Government Job

Before paying an application fee, applicants must carefully compare their profile against the official notification:
* **Education:** Do you hold the exact degree/certificate required?
* **Age:** Do you fall within the age limits as of the specified cut-off date?
* **Domicile:** Do you have a Maharashtra Domicile Certificate?
* **Category:** Do you possess a valid Caste Certificate and Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate if claiming reservation?
* **Experience:** Does the post require prior work experience?
* **Physical Standards:** Can you meet the height/chest requirements for uniformed services?
* **Language Requirements:** Almost all state jobs require a certificate proving proficiency in Marathi (usually reading, writing, and speaking). Use our [eligibility checker](/eligibility-checker) to track your baseline qualifications.
