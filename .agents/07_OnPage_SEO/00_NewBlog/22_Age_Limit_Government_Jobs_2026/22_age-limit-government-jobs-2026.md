# Age Limit for Government Jobs 2026: Rules, Relaxation & How to Check

Almost every **Sarkari Naukri** notification opens with two numbers: minimum age and maximum age, counted on a **specific date** (usually the closing date of application). One day over the limit means rejection at document verification — even if you cleared the written exam.

**Direct answer:** The **age limit for government jobs** is set separately for each recruitment in the official notification. Calculate your age as on the date mentioned in that PDF, then apply category-wise relaxation only if you have valid certificates and the notice allows it.

## Quick Facts

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Authority | Each recruiting body (UPSC, SSC, RRB, state PSC, etc.) |
| Age reference date | Stated in notification (often last date to apply) |
| Relaxation | SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/ex-servicemen — as per **that** notice |
| Tool | [Age calculator](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/age-calculator) |
| Final say | Official notification only |

## How age is calculated

1. Find “Age as on …” in the notification.
2. Use 10th certificate date of birth (unless notice allows other proof).
3. Count completed years on that date — not “running age” unless specified.
4. Compare with min/max for your category.

Use the age calculator for a first check; then manually confirm with the PDF table.

## Typical age bands (illustrative only)

Central exams often use 18–27 or 18–32 for general category **for many posts**, but UPSC, SSC, railway, and state notices differ widely. **Never assume** — read your advertisement.

| Category | Often seen pattern | Verify in PDF |
|----------|-------------------|---------------|
| General | Min 18, max 27–32 (post-specific) | Required |
| OBC (NCL) | +3 years relaxation (if allowed) | Required |
| SC/ST | +5 years (if allowed) | Required |
| PwBD | Post-wise extra relaxation | Required |
| Ex-servicemen | Rules in notification | Required |

## Age relaxation documents

- Valid caste certificate (central format if central job).
- EWS certificate if claiming EWS quota (where applicable).
- PwBD certificate with percentage of disability.
- Ex-servicemen discharge book / service certificate.

Mismatch between form category and certificate is a common disqualification.

## State vs central jobs

- **UPSC/SSC/RRB** — follow central rules in notification.
- **State PSC / police / ZP** — state-specific age and domicile common.

Maharashtra: see [Maharashtra government jobs](/blogs/maharashtra-government-jobs-2026) and [districts](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/districts).

## Step-by-step before you apply

1. Download official notification.
2. Note age reference date.
3. Run [age calculator](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/age-calculator).
4. Read relaxation table for your category.
5. Confirm certificates match.
6. Apply via [guide/government-jobs-2026](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/guide/government-jobs-2026).

## FAQ

**What is the maximum age for government jobs in 2026?** There is no single national maximum — each notification defines it.

**Does OBC get age relaxation?** Usually for Non-Creamy Layer if notification says so — certificate required.

**Is age counted on exam date or apply date?** Whichever date the PDF states — usually last date to apply.

**Can I apply if I am one day older than max age?** No — limits are strict unless court order/ corrigendum changes them.

**Does Search Sarkari Naukri set age limits?** No. We explain rules; the notification controls eligibility.
