# SSC CHSL 2026 Apply Online: Eligibility, Dates & Steps

Staff Selection Commission Combined Higher Secondary Level (CHSL) recruitment is one of the most searched **10+2 government job** exams in India. If you have passed Class 12 and want a central government clerical or assistant post, CHSL is often the first exam you should read about — but only after you open the **official notification** on [ssc.nic.in](https://ssc.nic.in).

**Direct answer:** To apply for **SSC CHSL 2026**, register on the SSC portal when the notification is active, confirm 10+2 eligibility and age on the closing date, upload photo and signature as per specs, pay the fee if applicable, and submit before the last date and time shown in the advertisement. Always use [ssc.nic.in](https://ssc.nic.in) — not third-party “apply here” links.

## Who can apply for SSC CHSL?

**Who:** Indian citizens (and other categories if notification allows) who meet **educational qualification**, **age limit**, and **nationality** rules in the active advertisement.

**What qualification:** Usually 12th pass or equivalent from a recognised board. Some posts may specify skill tests (typing/data entry) after selection — read the post-wise requirements.

**Age:** Minimum and maximum age are defined in the notification, with relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/ex-servicemen as per government rules **only if listed in that notice**.

Use the [age calculator](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/age-calculator) for a basic check, then match the PDF line by line.

## SSC CHSL selection pattern (overview)

Pattern can change by year. Typically CHSL includes:

1. **Tier-I** — Computer-based objective test (often reasoning, GA, quant, English).
2. **Tier-II** — Descriptive paper (where notified).
3. **Skill test / typing test** — For posts such as DEO/Data Entry (as applicable).
4. **Document verification** — Certificates must match the form exactly.

For subject-wise detail, see [exam syllabus and pattern](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/exam-syllabus-patterns) and SSC’s official syllabus for your notification year.

## Documents before you apply

- 10th certificate (DOB proof)
- 12th marksheet/certificate
- Category certificate (if claiming relaxation/fee exemption) in prescribed format
- PwBD certificate if applicable
- Photo and signature (dimensions and file size as per SSC portal)
- Valid ID (Aadhaar, voter ID, etc. as allowed)

Scanned files should be readable — blurred uploads are a common rejection reason.

## Step-by-step: SSC CHSL 2026 apply online

1. Open **https://ssc.nic.in** and find the active CHSL notification.
2. Download and read the full PDF (eligibility, posts, dates, fee).
3. Click **Apply** / **Registration** on the official SSC application portal linked from SSC only.
4. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) if you are new; use mobile and email you control.
5. Fill the CHSL application form — post preferences, category, education details.
6. Upload photo and signature; preview carefully.
7. Pay fee online if required (save receipt).
8. Submit and **save confirmation page / application PDF**.
9. Note registration number and password securely.

For a general government apply flow, see [Complete guide: government jobs 2026](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/guide/government-jobs-2026).

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Applying on a fake `.com` site mimicking SSC branding.
- Wrong category selected without valid certificate.
- Name spelling different from 10th certificate.
- Missing the **time** of last date (not just the date).
- Ignoring post-specific skill test requirements.

## How to verify before you pay the fee

- Notification PDF downloaded from **ssc.nic.in**.
- Application URL domain is SSC’s official application system.
- Advertisement number on the form matches the PDF.
- Last date matches PDF (check corrigendum).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is SSC CHSL?
SSC CHSL is Staff Selection Commission recruitment for higher secondary level posts in central government ministries and departments, commonly sought by 12th pass candidates.

### How do I apply for SSC CHSL 2026 online?
Register and apply only through the official SSC website and application portal linked from [ssc.nic.in](https://ssc.nic.in), following the active notification.

### What is the eligibility for SSC CHSL?
Usually 12th pass or equivalent; exact rules, age, and nationality are in the official notification for that year.

### Is there negative marking in SSC CHSL Tier-I?
If Tier-I is objective, negative marking is usually mentioned in the notification — read the scheme of examination for your cycle.

### Can I apply for SSC CHSL and SSC CGL both?
Yes, if you meet separate eligibility for each exam and exam dates do not conflict — check each notification.

### Where is the SSC CHSL 2026 last date?
Only in the official notification and SSC portal — verify there; do not trust forwarded messages.

### What posts come under CHSL?
Post names vary by notification (e.g. LDC, JSA, DEO, PA/SA) — see vacancy table in PDF.

### Is Search Sarkari Naukri official SSC?
No. We are an independent portal. Apply only on SSC’s official website.

### What documents are needed for SSC CHSL?
Typically 10th and 12th certificates, ID, photo, signature, and category/PwBD certificates if claimed — as per notification.
