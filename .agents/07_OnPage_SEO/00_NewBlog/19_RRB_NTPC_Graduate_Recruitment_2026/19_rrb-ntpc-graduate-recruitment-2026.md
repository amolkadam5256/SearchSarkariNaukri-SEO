# RRB NTPC Graduate Recruitment 2026: Apply, Eligibility & Official Links

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) **Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC)** graduate-level exams attract lakhs of applicants every cycle. Posts such as undergraduate-level clerical, station master (where notified), and other graduate categories are central **railway jobs** — but each CEN (Centralised Employment Notice) has its own vacancy table, age bar, and apply window.

**Direct answer:** For **RRB NTPC graduate recruitment 2026**, read the active CEN notification on the **official RRB website for your zone** (listed on [rrcb.gov.in](https://www.rrcb.gov.in)), apply only through the link in that notification, and verify graduate qualification and age on the closing date.

## What is RRB NTPC graduate level?

NTPC recruitments cover various non-technical popular category posts. **Graduate level** notices require a bachelor’s degree (or equivalent) from a recognised university unless the notification states otherwise. Posts and pay levels change by CEN — never assume last year’s post list applies.

## Who can apply?

- **Education:** Degree as defined in the CEN PDF.
- **Age:** Min/max on last date of application; relaxation only as per notification.
- **Nationality:** Indian citizen rules as in notice.
- **Medical / fitness:** Railway medical standards for relevant posts.

Check [eligibility checker](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/eligibility-checker) then confirm in PDF.

## How to apply (step-by-step)

1. Find the official CEN PDF (download from RRB site — not WhatsApp PDFs).
2. Confirm you apply on the **correct zonal RRB** website named in the notice.
3. Complete RRB registration / application as per CEN instructions.
4. Upload documents; pay fee if required.
5. Submit before deadline; save printout.

General apply tips: [guide/government-jobs-2026](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/guide/government-jobs-2026).

## Exam pattern (overview)

Typical stages (confirm in your CEN):

- **CBT-1 / CBT-2** — Objective papers (GA, maths, reasoning, etc. as notified).
- **Typing / skill test** — If applicable to post.
- **Document verification & medical**.

## FAQ

### When does RRB NTPC graduate application start?
Only the active CEN notification on official RRB sites states open and close dates.

### Can 12th pass apply for graduate NTPC?
No for graduate posts — you need degree eligibility unless a separate 12th-level CEN exists.

### Which RRB website should I use?
The one specified in **your** notification (zone-specific).

### Is NTPC the same as Group D?
No. Group D (Level 1) and NTPC are different recruitments with different eligibility.

### Does Search Sarkari Naukri conduct railway recruitment?
No. We aggregate information; RRB conducts recruitment.
