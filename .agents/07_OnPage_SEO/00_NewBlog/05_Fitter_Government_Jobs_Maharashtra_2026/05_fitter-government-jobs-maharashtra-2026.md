# Fitter Government Jobs in Maharashtra 2026: Railway, Workshop & PSU Opportunities

An ITI Fitter certificate opens specific government job opportunities in Maharashtra that focus on mechanical fitting, workshop maintenance, and technical assembly work.

The mechanical sector—railway workshops, government factories, manufacturing units, and technical establishments—regularly recruits candidates with Fitter trade qualifications. The challenge is knowing which workshops and organisations recruit Fitters, what vacancies are available, and how to target the right opportunities.

ITI Fitter candidates can apply for Railway Workshop Fitter positions, government manufacturing unit mechanical roles, PSU technical assembly positions, government workshop maintenance staff, and technical establishment Fitter posts. Eligibility depends on the specific trade requirement in each recruitment notification.

## What are Fitter government jobs in Maharashtra?

Fitter government jobs are technical positions in government and public-sector organisations that specifically require ITI Fitter qualifications for mechanical fitting and assembly work.

Depending on the recruitment, Fitter candidates may find opportunities in:

* Railway workshops
* Government manufacturing units
* Public-sector companies
* Technical workshops
* Maintenance departments
* Engineering establishments
* Defence workshops
* PSUs with mechanical operations

The exact qualification depends on the post.

Some vacancies may require only 10th plus ITI Fitter, while others may ask for additional mechanical certifications, experience, or specific trade percentages.

## Apprenticeship vs permanent Fitter jobs

This is one of the most important distinctions for Fitter candidates.

### Fitter apprenticeship

* Practical fitting training
* Workshop experience
* Trade skill development
* Certificate completion

### Permanent Fitter recruitment
A regular government recruitment post may offer:

* Permanent or regular employment
* Defined service conditions
* Government salary structure

Do not assume:

Apprenticeship = permanent government job

## How selection works for Fitter positions

There is no single selection process for all Fitter government jobs.

Depending on the organisation and post, selection may involve:

### Merit-based selection
Some apprenticeship recruitments use marks-based merit or another merit process.

### Written examination
Some technical government posts include a written test on mechanical knowledge.

### Trade or skill test
Certain mechanical positions may require candidates to demonstrate practical fitting skills.

### Document verification
Shortlisted candidates may have to produce original documents.

### Medical examination
Some posts, particularly in workshops or manufacturing units, may include medical standards.

Read the selection process before starting preparation.

If there is no written examination, spending months preparing for a competitive exam may not be necessary for that particular recruitment.

## Workshop-based vs field-based Fitter jobs

Fitter candidates should understand the difference between workshop-based and field-based positions.

### Workshop-based Fitter jobs
Workshop-based positions typically involve:

* Indoor workshop work
* Equipment assembly and maintenance
* Machinery fitting
* Controlled environment work
* Regular working hours

### Field-based Fitter jobs
Field-based positions may involve:

* On-site maintenance work
* Equipment installation at different locations
* Travel to work sites
* Variable working conditions
* Different work schedules

Candidates should check the notification to understand whether the position is workshop-based or field-based.

## Fitter vs other mechanical trades

Some notifications may mention multiple mechanical trades.

### Common mechanical trade variations
* Fitter
* Mechanical Fitter
* General Fitter
* Maintenance Fitter
* Workshop Fitter

Some notifications accept multiple trades, while others specify one.

Always check whether the notification says:

* Fitter only
* Mechanical Fitter only
* Fitter/other mechanical trades
* General mechanical trade

Do not assume that your mechanical trade automatically qualifies for every "mechanical" vacancy.

## How to verify a government job before applying

Scams and fake job alerts are common. Applicants must verify the authenticity of a job alert before paying any fees or submitting personal details.

Check the **official notification** on the **official recruitment authority** website (URLs usually ending in `.gov.in` or `.nic.in`). Verify the **application URL** to ensure you are not on a lookalike site. Read the official PDF to confirm the **deadline**, **fee**, **eligibility**, **vacancy** count, and the exact **selection process**. Never pay money to unauthorized agents promising government jobs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Which government jobs can ITI Fitters apply for in Maharashtra?**
ITI Fitter candidates can apply for Railway Workshop Fitter positions, government manufacturing unit mechanical roles, PSU technical assembly positions, government workshop maintenance staff, and technical establishment Fitter posts.

**2. Can I get a government job after ITI Fitter without a diploma?**
Yes. Some government and public-sector mechanical vacancies accept ITI Fitter qualifications without requiring a diploma, but this depends on the specific post and notification requirements.

**3. What is the difference between workshop-based and field-based Fitter jobs?**
Workshop-based Fitter jobs involve indoor workshop work with equipment assembly and maintenance in controlled environments, while field-based positions involve on-site maintenance work, equipment installation at different locations, and variable working conditions.

**4. Does Railway recruit ITI Fitter candidates?**
Yes. Railway workshops are one of the largest recruiters for ITI Fitter candidates, offering Fitter (workshop), Mechanical Fitter, Apprentice Fitter, and Technical Helper positions.

**5. Are there Fitter government jobs in Nagpur?**
Yes. Nagpur has mechanical recruitment opportunities through railway workshops in Nagpur region, government manufacturing units, PSU mechanical departments, and government technical establishments.

**6. What documents are required for Fitter government jobs?**
Generally, you need 10th marksheet, ITI Fitter marksheet and certificate, trade certificate, Aadhaar card, photograph, signature, and caste/category certificate if claiming reservation.

**7. What is the selection process for Fitter positions?**
Selection processes vary but may include merit-based selection for apprenticeships, written examinations for technical posts, trade tests for practical fitting skills, document verification, and medical examinations.

**8. Can other mechanical trades apply for Fitter vacancies?**
Some notifications accept multiple mechanical trades while others specify Fitter only. Always check the exact trade eligibility in the official notification before applying.

**9. Are there Fitter opportunities in PSUs?**
Yes. PSUs with mechanical operations recruit Fitter candidates for manufacturing operations, mechanical maintenance, workshop operations, and technical assembly roles.

**10. How can I find the latest Fitter government vacancies?**
You can find the latest verified vacancies on official government portals like Railway recruitment websites, PSU portals, and government manufacturing unit websites, or by checking the Job Updates section on the Search Sarkari Naukri website.
