Pune is one of Maharashtra's most important cities for ITI candidates seeking government jobs.

The city has MAHA Metro projects, government workshops, technical establishments, municipal corporations, and various government organisations that recruit ITI-qualified candidates. If you have completed ITI and are looking for a government job in Pune, the right approach is to focus on Pune-based organisations, monitor Pune-specific recruitment, and understand the local job market rather than applying for every Maharashtra government vacancy.
