## Which Government Jobs Are Available After 10th?

A Class 10 certificate opens doors to entry-level roles in multiple government sectors. These are generally classified under Group C and Group D categories. The responsibilities often include assisting senior staff, maintaining records, field duties, and facility maintenance.

### Railway
The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) and Railway Recruitment Cells (RRC) are among the largest employers for 10th-pass candidates. Roles include Track Maintainer Grade-IV, Helper/Assistant in various technical departments (Electrical, Mechanical, Engineering), and Assistant Pointsman. Some technical roles in railways may require an ITI certificate along with the 10th pass mark sheet.

### India Post
India Post regularly hires Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS), Branch Postmaster (BPM), and Assistant Branch Postmaster (ABPM). Recruitment for these roles is usually merit-based, relying directly on the marks obtained in Class 10 without a separate written exam.

### Police
Many state police departments recruit Constables, Armed Reserve Police, and Drivers based on a 10th pass qualification. Physical fitness and endurance tests are strictly enforced for these roles. Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) also recruit for General Duty (GD) Constable posts, managed through the SSC GD examination.

### Defence
The Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force recruit 10th-pass candidates for non-combatant roles such as Tradesmen, Safaiwala, Washerman, and Mess Staff. Agniveer recruitment rallies also feature opportunities for candidates who have completed Class 10.

### State Government Departments
Various state ministries and municipal corporations conduct recruitment for Peons, Watchmen, Sweepers, and Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS). These are permanent state government positions with standard pay scale benefits. Find updates on our [jobs board](/jobs).

## 10th Pass Government Jobs by Department

Different government departments handle their own recruitment processes. Here is a breakdown of major recruiting bodies:

### Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
SSC conducts the Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff Examination annually. This is a highly sought-after central government job. Candidates must pass a computer-based examination.

### State Public Service Commissions
While Public Service Commissions usually hire for higher-level officer posts, they occasionally oversee subordinate services selection boards that handle Group C and Group D recruitment for state ministries.

### Municipal Corporations
Local city councils and municipal bodies frequently release notifications for public health workers, ward boys, and administrative assistants. These are typically localized recruitments favoring candidates from the specific district.

## 10th Pass Government Jobs by State

A candidate applying for a state-specific job must usually prove local language proficiency and, in many cases, provide a domicile certificate.

State-level exams are handled by local authorities, while central jobs (like Post Office or Railways) are open nationwide, though postings can be regional. ## Eligibility for 10th Pass Government Jobs

Meeting the baseline eligibility criteria is mandatory before filling out an application form. If a candidate applies without meeting these requirements, the application will be rejected during document verification.

### Educational Qualification
The primary requirement is a Class 10 passing certificate from a recognized state or central board (such as CBSE, ICSE, or State SSC board). Ensure that the date of passing matches the cut-off date mentioned in the official notification.

### Age Requirement
The minimum age for most 10th pass government jobs is 18 years. The maximum age generally ranges from 25 to 30 years, depending on the post and department.

### Age Relaxation
Reserved categories receive standard age relaxations under government norms:
* OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): Usually 3 years
* SC/ST: Usually 5 years
* PwD: 10 years or more, depending on the specific disability and category.

### Nationality and Domicile
Applicants must be citizens of India. For state-level police or municipal jobs, candidates often need a domicile certificate proving they are residents of that specific state.

### Physical Requirements
Police, Defence, and some Railway posts have strict physical standards regarding height, chest measurement (for males), and weight. Candidates also have to pass physical efficiency tests like running or long jump.

### Additional Qualifications
While the base qualification is 10th pass, specific roles (like Railway technical staff) might ask for an ITI diploma. Driving posts will require a valid driving license. Use our [eligibility checker](/eligibility-checker) to verify your qualifications against current openings.

## Salary for 10th Pass Government Jobs

The salary for a 10th pass government job varies based on the department, pay level, and city of posting. Most 10th pass entry-level jobs fall under Pay Level 1 of the 7th Pay Commission.

The starting basic pay is typically ₹18,000 per month. On top of the basic pay, government employees receive Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), and Transport Allowance (TA). Depending on the location (Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3 city), the gross monthly salary usually ranges between ₹25,000 and ₹32,000. 

For roles like Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS), the compensation is provided as Time Related Continuity Allowance (TRCA), which usually ranges from ₹10,000 to ₹14,500 basic, plus applicable allowances.

Always verify the exact file size and format required in the official notification.

## How to Apply for a 10th Pass Government Job

Follow this step-by-step process to ensure a successful application:

1. **Find the recruitment notification:** Look for official announcements on the department's website or check reliable updates.
2. **Read eligibility:** Confirm you meet the age and educational requirements.
3. **Check important dates:** Note the opening and closing dates for online registration. Missing the deadline means missing the opportunity.
4. **Download the notification:** Keep a PDF copy for reference regarding syllabus and physical requirements.
5. **Open the official application link:** Always use the URL provided by the official government authority.
6. **Register:** Create an account using your email ID and mobile number.
7. **Enter details carefully:** Fill in your name exactly as it appears on your 10th certificate. 
8. **Upload documents:** Upload your photograph, signature, and certificates in the correct dimensions.
9. **Pay the fee if applicable:** Use net banking, UPI, or debit/credit cards. Many departments exempt female and SC/ST candidates from application fees.
10. **Submit the application:** Review all details before final submission.
11. **Save the application/receipt:** Download and print the final confirmation page for future reference.

## How to Verify a Government Job Before Applying

Scams and fake job alerts are common. Applicants must verify the authenticity of a job alert before paying any fees or submitting personal details.

Check the **official notification** on the **official recruitment authority** website (URLs usually ending in `.gov.in` or `.nic.in`). Verify the **application URL** to ensure you are not on a lookalike site. Read the official PDF to confirm the **deadline**, **fee**, **eligibility**, **vacancy** count, and the exact **selection process**. Never pay money to unauthorized agents promising government jobs.
