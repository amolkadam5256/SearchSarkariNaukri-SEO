## How to verify a government job before applying

Scams and fake job alerts are common. Applicants must verify the authenticity of a job alert before paying any fees or submitting personal details.

Check the **official notification** on the **official recruitment authority** website (URLs usually ending in `.gov.in` or `.nic.in`). Verify the **application URL** to ensure you are not on a lookalike site. Read the official PDF to confirm the **deadline**, **fee**, **eligibility**, **vacancy** count, and the exact **selection process**. Never pay money to unauthorized agents promising government jobs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Which government jobs can ITI candidates apply for in Pune?**
ITI candidates in Pune can apply for MAHA Metro Pune Technician positions, Pune Municipal Corporation technical staff, government workshop maintenance roles, state government technical positions, and central government organisation technical vacancies in Pune.

**2. Can I get a government job in Pune after ITI without a diploma?**
Yes. Some government and public-sector technical vacancies in Pune accept ITI qualifications without requiring a diploma, but this depends on the specific post and notification requirements.

**3. Does MAHA Metro Pune recruit ITI candidates?**
Yes. MAHA Metro Pune project is a major recruiter for ITI candidates, offering Electrical Technician, Mechanical Technician, Maintenance Technician, and other technical positions for various ITI trades.

**4. What is the difference between Pune city and Pune district jobs?**
Pune city jobs are located within Pune municipal limits and include MAHA Metro Pune and PMC positions, while Pune district jobs include opportunities in rural areas, taluka-level offices, and district-level technical positions.

**5. Do Pune government jobs require domicile certificates?**
Some Pune government jobs may have domicile requirements specifying Pune city domicile, Pune district domicile, Maharashtra domicile, or no domicile requirement. Check the specific requirement in the official notification.

**6. What documents are required for Pune government jobs?**
Generally, you need 10th marksheet and certificate, ITI marksheet and certificate, trade certificate, Aadhaar card, photograph, signature, and Pune domicile certificate if required by the notification.

**7. How can I find the latest Pune ITI government vacancies?**
You can find the latest verified vacancies on official organisation websites like MAHA Metro Pune, PMC, and government workshop portals, or by checking the Job Updates section on the Search Sarkari Naukri website.

**8. What is the salary for Pune ITI government jobs?**
Pune ITI positions typically fall under Level 1 (₹18,000-56,900) for Group D positions and Level 2 (₹19,900-63,200) for Group C positions, plus Pune-specific dearness allowance and house rent allowance.

**9. Does PMC recruit ITI candidates?**
Yes. Pune Municipal Corporation can recruit ITI candidates for municipal technical staff positions in electrical, mechanical, maintenance, and technical support departments.

**10. What is the selection process for Pune ITI government jobs?**
Selection processes vary but may include written examinations, trade tests, skill tests, interviews, document verification, and medical examinations depending on the organisation and position.
