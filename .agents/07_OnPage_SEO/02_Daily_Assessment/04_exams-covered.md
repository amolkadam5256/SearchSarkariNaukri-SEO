# 04 — Exams Covered

## H2
Exams Covered in the Daily Assessment Program

## Program Scope
The daily assessment is organized around the structural requirements of major competitive exams, offering specialized tracks for civil services along with relevant practice for wider government recruitment.

## Primary Exam Focus
- **UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE Prelims)** — Targeted practice for General Studies Paper I, focusing on national current affairs, polity, history, geography, economy, and environmental science.  
  Explore full exam details: [UPSC Civil Services Guide](/exams/upsc-cse)
- **MPSC Rajyaseva & State Services** — Comprehensive coverage combining national general studies with Maharashtra-specific history, geography, governance, and state welfare schemes.  
  Explore full exam details: [MPSC Rajyaseva Guide](/exams/mpsc-rajyaseva)

## Supplementary Exam Value
General awareness and current events are core components across many recruitment examinations. Aspirants preparing for the following exams can use the daily question bank to supplement their general preparation:
- **Staff Selection Commission** — [SSC CGL & CHSL General Awareness](/exams/ssc-cgl)
- **Railway Recruitment Boards** — [RRB NTPC & Group D General Knowledge](/exams/rrb-ntpc)
- **Banking Recruitment** — [SBI PO & IBPS General / Financial Awareness](/exams/sbi-po-clerk)
- **State Recruitment Boards** — [Maharashtra Police Bharti](/exams/maharashtra-police-bharti), Talathi Bharti, and State Forest Guard examinations via our [Exams Directory](/exams).
