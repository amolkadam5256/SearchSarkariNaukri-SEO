### Question 1: Indian Polity — Constitutional Bodies
**Which constitutional body in India is tasked under Article 324 with superintendence, direction, and control of elections to Parliament and state legislatures?**
- A) Delimitation Commission
- B) Election Commission of India
- C) Union Public Service Commission
- D) Law Commission of India

- **Correct Answer**: B) Election Commission of India
- **Explanation**: Article 324 of the Indian Constitution vests the superintendence, direction, and control of the preparation of electoral rolls and the conduct of all elections to Parliament and to the Legislature of every State in the Election Commission of India.
- **Topic**: Indian Polity & Governance
- **Subtopic**: Constitutional Bodies
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Exam Relevance**: UPSC Civil Services Prelims (GS Paper I), MPSC Rajyaseva
- **Official Source**: Constitution of India (Part XV, Article 324)
- **Reviewed**: 10 September 2026

### Question 2: Current Affairs — International Agreements & Trade
**Under India's international maritime strategy, which port development project in Iran serves as a crucial transit corridor to Central Asian republics and Russia via the INSTC (International North–South Transport Corridor)?**
- A) Bandar Abbas Port
- B) Chabahar Port (Shahid Beheshti Terminal)
- C) Gwadar Port
- D) Salalah Port

- **Correct Answer**: B) Chabahar Port (Shahid Beheshti Terminal)
- **Explanation**: India entered a long-term agreement for the management and development of the Shahid Beheshti terminal at Chabahar Port in Iran. It provides strategic sea-land access to Afghanistan and Central Asia, bypassing overland routes through Pakistan, and serves as an anchor node for the International North–South Transport Corridor (INSTC).
- **Topic**: Current Affairs & International Relations
- **Subtopic**: Infrastructure & Strategic Corridors
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Exam Relevance**: UPSC CSE Prelims, MPSC State Services
- **Official Source**: Ministry of External Affairs / Press Information Bureau (PIB)
- **Reviewed**: 10 September 2026

### Question 3: Maharashtra GK — State Administration & Welfare
**Under the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, what is the primary objective established for designated public authorities across state departments?**
- A) Mandatory privatization of municipal administrative services
- B) Providing notified citizen services in a transparent, accountable, and time-bound manner
- C) Automatic transfer of revenue officers every two years
- D) Replacement of physical collectorates with district-level call centers

- **Correct Answer**: B) Providing notified citizen services in a transparent, accountable, and time-bound manner
- **Explanation**: The Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act was enacted to ensure that citizens receive notified public services from government departments in a transparent, efficient, and time-bound environment, with provision for first and second appellate mechanisms for delayed service delivery.
- **Topic**: Maharashtra GK & Administration
- **Subtopic**: Governance & Citizen Charters
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Exam Relevance**: MPSC Rajyaseva, MPSC Combined Group B & C
- **Official Source**: Government of Maharashtra Gazette (General Administration Dept.)
- **Reviewed**: 10 September 2026

### Question 4: Indian Economy — Monetary Policy & Banking
**What term describes the interest rate at which the Reserve Bank of India absorbs liquidity from commercial banks against eligible government securities on an overnight basis?**
- A) Repo Rate
- B) Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) Rate
- C) Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) Rate
- D) Bank Rate

- **Correct Answer**: B) Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) Rate
- **Explanation**: The Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) allows the RBI to absorb overnight liquidity from commercial banks without requiring the collateral of government securities, operating at a spread below the policy Repo Rate as the lower bound of the liquidity adjustment facility (LAF) corridor.
- **Topic**: Indian Economy
- **Subtopic**: Monetary Policy Framework
- **Difficulty**: Moderate to High
- **Exam Relevance**: UPSC CSE Prelims, MPSC General Studies
- **Official Source**: Reserve Bank of India (RBI Monetary Policy Framework)
- **Reviewed**: 10 September 2026

### Question 5: Environment & Geography — River Systems
**The Kosi river, frequently associated with severe seasonal flooding and course shifting in northern Bihar, originates in which Himalayan region before joining the Ganga?**
- A) Mansarovar Lake region in Tibet
- B) Glaciers of the Greater Himalayas in Tibet and Nepal (comprising the Sun Kosi, Arun, and Tamur)
- C) Zanskar Range in Ladakh
- D) Rohtang Pass in Himachal Pradesh

- **Correct Answer**: B) Glaciers of the Greater Himalayas in Tibet and Nepal (comprising the Sun Kosi, Arun, and Tamur)
- **Explanation**: The Kosi is a trans-boundary river originating from the northern slopes of Mount Everest in Tibet and the eastern Himalayas of Nepal. Its three primary tributaries—the Sun Kosi, Arun, and Tamur—join together at the Chatra Gorge to form the Saptakoshi before entering the plains of northern Bihar.
- **Topic**: Geography & Physical Systems
- **Subtopic**: Drainage Systems of India
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Exam Relevance**: UPSC Prelims, MPSC General Studies
- **Official Source**: Survey of India / Central Water Commission
- **Reviewed**: 10 September 2026
