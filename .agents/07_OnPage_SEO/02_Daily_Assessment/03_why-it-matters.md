# 03 — Why Daily Assessment Matters

## H2
Why Daily Assessments Are Important for UPSC, MPSC & Government Exam Preparation

## Intro
A daily current affairs quiz and syllabus practice routine is one of the most consistent ways to prepare for competitive government exams. Ten minutes a day compounds into real exam readiness.

## Points (H3 cards)
- **Improves retention** — Daily recall of current affairs and syllabus topics moves facts from short-term to long-term memory.
- **Improves revision** — Regular government exam practice questions reinforce what you studied instead of letting it fade.
- **Builds consistency** — A daily UPSC and MPSC quiz habit is easier to sustain than occasional long mock tests.
- **Identifies weak topics** — Explanations after every question show exactly where your preparation needs more attention.
