# 09 — Preparation Strategy

## H2
How to Use Daily Assessment for UPSC & MPSC Preparation

## Steps
1. **Read current affairs** — Skim the day's important news before attempting the quiz.
2. **Solve the quiz** — Complete all 10 questions without skipping, in under 12 minutes.
3. **Review mistakes** — Read every explanation, especially for questions you got wrong.
4. **Revise & repeat** — Note weak topics, revise them, and return tomorrow to keep your streak going.
