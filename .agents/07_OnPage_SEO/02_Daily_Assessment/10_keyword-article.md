Daily UPSC, MPSC, SSC, Railway and Government Exam Practice Questions

Consistent practice is one of the biggest differentiators between aspirants who clear competitive government exams and those who don't. Each day's UPSC daily quiz combines five current-affairs questions drawn from the previous week's news with five syllabus-based questions covering Polity, History, Geography, Economy, Science and Environment — the same subject spread tested in the UPSC Civil Services Examination preliminary paper. This means every daily session doubles as both a government exam mock test and a current-affairs refresher, without requiring a separate study routine for each.

For MPSC Rajyaseva and other Maharashtra state exam aspirants, the MPSC daily quiz adds Maharashtra GK, state government schemes and region-specific current affairs alongside the national syllabus. This makes it one of the few daily gk questions resources that genuinely serves both UPSC and MPSC aspirants from a single dashboard.

### Practice questions beyond UPSC and MPSC
The same question bank also supports SSC preparation, Railway RRB exams, Banking (SBI/IBPS) recruitment, Maharashtra Police Bharti, Talathi Bharti, Forest Guard and Gram Sevak exams. ### Why a daily mock test beats occasional long tests
Most government exam practice test platforms push long, infrequent mock exams. Research on spaced repetition and active recall shows that short, frequent practice — like a daily current affairs questions routine — improves retention far more effectively than cramming. Because Search Sarkari Naukri never repeats a question, every day's assessment adds genuinely new material to your revision instead of testing the same facts again.

Whether you are just starting your UPSC preparation or are in the final months before your MPSC preparation exam, ten focused minutes a day with the Daily Assessment keeps your current affairs sharp, your syllabus revision active, and your exam-day confidence steadily building.
