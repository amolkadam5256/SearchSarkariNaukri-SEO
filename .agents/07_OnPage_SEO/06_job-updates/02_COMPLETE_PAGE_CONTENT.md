### SECTION 15 — How to Check a Government Job Before Applying

**H2:** How Should You Check a Government Job Before Applying?

Before submitting any government job application, verify these points in the official recruitment notification:

1. **Eligibility** — Qualification, age limit, nationality, experience and category-specific requirements
2. **Important dates** — Application start date, last date, fee payment deadline, exam date
3. **Application fee** — Amount, payment mode, fee exemption for reserved categories
4. **Vacancy details** — Post name, number of posts, pay scale, job location
5. **Required documents** — Certificates, ID proof, photographs, signatures (size/format)
6. **Selection process** — Written exam, physical test, interview, document verification
7. **Official application website** — Confirm URL matches the recruiting authority's domain
8. **Official notification PDF** — Download and read the full advertisement

**Helpful tools on Search Sarkari Naukri:**
- [Eligibility Checker](/eligibility-checker) — basic profile matching
- [Age Calculator](/age-calculator) — age limit calculation
- [Exam Calendar](/exam-calendar) — upcoming exam dates

Search Sarkari Naukri collects recruitment information from official notifications and official recruitment websites published by government departments and public-sector organizations.
