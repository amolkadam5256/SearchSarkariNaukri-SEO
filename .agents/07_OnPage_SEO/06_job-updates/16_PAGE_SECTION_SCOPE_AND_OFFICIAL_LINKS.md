## PART C — SOURCE VERIFICATION HIERARCHY

Use this hierarchy on every job update published on Search Sarkari Naukri:

| Level | Source Type | Examples | Trust Level |
|-------|-------------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Official Recruitment Authority | UPSC, SSC, RRB, MPSC, SBI, IBPS, RBI, ISRO, DRDO, India Post | **Highest — always verify here before applying** |
| 2 | Government Portal | India.gov.in, NCS, Employment News, MyGov | High — recruitment directory |
| 3 | Government Announcements | PIB press releases | Medium-high — news/announcement context |
| 4 | Search Sarkari Naukri Editorial | Organized summary, eligibility breakdown, dates | Value-add — never replace Level 1 |

**Never use:** Random blogs, copied Telegram/WhatsApp forwards, unverified third-party job sites as "official source."

**Trust statement (required on page):**
> Search Sarkari Naukri is an independent government-job information platform. We are not affiliated with the Government of India, UPSC, SSC, Railway Recruitment Boards, MPSC or any other government recruitment authority. Always verify recruitment information through the official notification and official website before applying.
