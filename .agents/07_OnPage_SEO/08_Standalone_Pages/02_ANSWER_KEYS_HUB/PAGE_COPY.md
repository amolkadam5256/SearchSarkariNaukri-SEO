Sarkari Exam Answer Key 2026 — Official Keys and Objection Dates

## Intro

After many government exams, the commission releases a **provisional answer key** so candidates can estimate scores before the result. If you think a question or key is wrong, there is usually a short **objection window** on the official portal — with a fee per question in some exams.

We list answer key status and official URLs when they are published. We do not publish unofficial keys. For final marks, always wait for the **final answer key** and the **result** on the commission’s site.

---

## H2 — Quick answer: how do answer keys work?

The exam authority uploads a key matching the question paper set or shift. You compare your responses with the key. If the commission accepts your objection, the key is revised and the result is calculated on the final key.

## H2 — Answer keys by recruiting body

### SSC (Staff Selection Commission)

SSC releases keys for CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD, CPO and other tiers on ssc.gov.in. Objection rules and fees are in the specific notice.

→ SSC jobs and updates: `/department/ssc`  
→ SSC results: `/results` (filter or search SSC)

### UPSC

UPSC publishes keys for eligible examinations on upsc.gov.in with a defined representation period.

→ `/department/upsc`

### Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB)

RRB exams such as NTPC and Group D publish keys on the regional RRB or central RRB sites listed in your admit card.

→ `/category/railway-jobs`

### IBPS and banks

IBPS PO, Clerk, SO and RRB exams use ibps.in; some banks publish separate keys for specialist recruitment.

→ `/category/banking-jobs`

### MPSC and Maharashtra state exams

State commissions publish keys on mpsc.gov.in and respective department portals.

→ `/department/mpsc`

## H2 — How to submit an answer key objection (general steps)

1. Open the official link from your admit card or post-exam notice.  
2. Log in with the same registration ID used in the exam.  
3. Select the question(s) you challenge and upload proof (textbook reference, official source).  
4. Pay the fee if required and save the receipt.  
5. Watch for the **final key** and then the **result**.

Exact steps change per exam — follow the PDF for that examination only.

## FAQ (visible = schema)

### Where can I download the SSC CGL answer key?

Only from ssc.gov.in or the link given in the official notice. We link out when the key is live.

### Is the provisional answer key final?

No. The result is based on the final key after objections are reviewed.

### Can SearchSarkariNaukri file objections for me?

No. Objections are submitted by the candidate on the official portal.

### What if there is no answer key for my exam?

Some interviews or skill tests do not use OMR keys. The selection list is released directly as a result or merit list.
