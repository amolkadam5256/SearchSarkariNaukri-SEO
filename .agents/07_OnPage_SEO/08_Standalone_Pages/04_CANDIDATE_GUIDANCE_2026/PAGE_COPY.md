## Intro

Government jobs still run on **notifications**, not on informal WhatsApp forwards. A valid recruitment has an advertisement number, a clear last date, eligibility written in plain language, and an apply link on a `.gov.in`, `.nic.in`, `.ac.in`, or authorised bank or PSU portal. Everything else is just rumour until you verify it.

SearchSarkariNaukri collects information from public sources so you can compare posts by state, qualification, and exam. **We are not an employer.** When you apply, you apply on the official site named in the PDF. This guide walks through that process calmly — the same steps whether you target SSC, a railway job, a bank clerk post, or MPSC in Maharashtra.

## H2 — Step 1: Find the right notification

Start with what you already have: qualification, age, category, and preferred state or department. Use:

- All jobs → `/jobs`  
- Qualification pages (10th, 12th, graduate) → linked from homepage  
- State directory → `/state-government-jobs`  
- Department pages (MPSC, SSC, UPSC) → `/department/...`  

Open the vacancy detail on our site, then **download or open the official notification** linked there. Read the post name carefully — similar titles can mean different grades or different states.

---

## H2 — Step 2: Check eligibility before you pay any fee

Most rejections happen because of age, qualification, or category certificate issues. Confirm:

- Minimum and maximum age on the closing date  
- Required degree or trade certificate  
- Experience if the post is not fresher-friendly  
- State domicile or language requirement  
- Physical standards for police, forest, or defence posts  

Use our eligibility tool if the post is listed: `/eligibility-checker` — then re-check the PDF line by line.

---

## H2 — Step 3: Documents you should keep ready

Scan and name files clearly before the apply window closes:

- 10th certificate for date of birth  
- Highest qualification marksheets  
- Caste or EWS certificate in the format the notice demands  
- PwBD certificate if applicable  
- Domicile or state certificate for state posts  
- Passport photo and signature within KB limits stated in the notice  
- ID proof (Aadhaar, voter ID, etc.) as allowed  

Keep originals for document verification later. If the notice asks for an affidavit or self-declaration, use the exact wording they provide.

---

## H2 — Step 4: Apply on the official portal only

Typical flow:

1. Register on the commission site (one-time registration for SSC/UPSC/RRB where applicable).  
2. Fill the application form for the specific advertisement.  
3. Upload photo and signature.  
4. Pay the fee online or via challan if offline fee is allowed.  
5. Submit and **save the confirmation page and application PDF**.  

Do not pay anyone who promises a job on our site or on social media. Fees go only to the account named in the notification.

---

## H2 — Step 5: After you apply — admit card, exam, result

- Admit card → `/admit-cards` and the official portal  
- Answer key → `/answer-keys`  
- Result → `/results`  

If you miss an exam, there is usually no re-exam in the same cycle. If the commission cancels a paper, follow only their corrigendum.

## H2 — How to spot a fake notification

- No advertisement number or employer name  
- Apply link goes to a random `.com` form with no `.gov.in` parent site  
- Someone asks for money to “guarantee selection”  
- Last date or vacancy count changes every day on social media but not on the official site  

We describe our own checks on `/recruitment-verification-process`. You should still open the PDF yourself.

## H2 — Maharashtra candidates — a few extra notes

Maharashtra runs parallel tracks: MPSC, police recruitment, Talathi and revenue posts, Zilla Parishad, health, and university ad-hoc posts. Dates often overlap. Use district pages (Pune, Mumbai, Nagpur, etc.) to narrow location, but verify posting district in the PDF — not every “Maharashtra” job is transferable to your home district.

## FAQ

### Can I apply for more than one government job at the same time?

Yes, if exam dates and eligibility allow. Watch for clauses that bar holding two appointments.

### Is there an age relaxation for OBC, SC, ST, or PwBD?

Usually yes, but only as defined in **that** notice. Read the relaxation table in the PDF.

### Do all government jobs have a written exam?

No. Some apprenticeships, walk-ins, or contract posts use interview or merit lists only.
