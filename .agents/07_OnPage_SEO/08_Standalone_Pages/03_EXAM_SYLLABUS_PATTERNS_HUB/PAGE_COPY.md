## Intro

Every commission publishes a **syllabus and scheme of examination** with the notification. The pattern tells you how many papers you face, the marks, time limits, and whether there is negative marking. The syllabus lists subjects and topics — that is what your preparation plan should follow.

Below is a sector-wise entry point. Each card links to deeper exam pages on SearchSarkariNaukri and, where possible, the **official syllabus PDF** on the commission website. If the notification changes mid-cycle, the official PDF wins over any summary here.

## FAQ

### Is the syllabus the same every year?

Broad subjects stay similar; commissions may tweak topics. Always download the syllabus attached to **your** notification year.

### Where is the MPSC Rajyaseva syllabus?

On mpsc.gov.in for the current cycle. Our MPSC department page links active exams.
