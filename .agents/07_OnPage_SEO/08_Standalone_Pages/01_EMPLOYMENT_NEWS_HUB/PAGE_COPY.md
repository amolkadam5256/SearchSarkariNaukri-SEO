## Intro (under H1)

Employment News (Rozgar Samachar) is the Government of India’s weekly publication for job openings in ministries, departments, PSUs, banks, railways, universities, and defence organisations. Many candidates still discover vacancies here first, especially outside metro cities.

SearchSarkariNaukri is **not** Employment News and **not** a government site. We summarise recruitments that appear in public notifications so you can compare last dates and eligibility in one place. When you decide to apply, use the **official PDF and the recruiting body’s website** — that document controls everything: posts, fees, age, and the closing date.

---

## H2 — Quick answer: what is Employment News?

Employment News is an official weekly recruitment magazine published for job seekers in India. Each issue lists active advertisements from government employers. The web edition is hosted at employmentnews.gov.in. Candidates use it to find notifications they may miss on individual department sites.

## H2 — How to use Employment News with SearchSarkariNaukri

1. Note the post name and advertisement number from the PDF.  
2. Search the organisation or post on our jobs page.  
3. Open the vacancy detail and read last date, age, and qualification.  
4. Download the official notification from the link we provide (if available).  
5. Apply only on the URL mentioned in that PDF — not on third-party forms.

## H2 — Frequently asked questions

### Is Employment News free?

Yes. The official website and authorised outlets provide access to issues; always use the government domain for downloads.

### Does SearchSarkariNaukri republish the full PDF?

No. We point to official sources and host structured summaries for active recruitments where we have verified data.

### How is this different from the Employment News website?

Employment News is the publisher. We are an independent portal that helps you search by state, qualification, exam, and department — especially if you follow Maharashtra and all-India exams together.

### Can I apply on SearchSarkariNaukri?

No. Application happens on the recruiting authority’s site or offline address given in the notification.

### How often should I check for new issues?

Employment News is weekly. For time-sensitive posts, also use our job alerts on WhatsApp or Telegram if you subscribe on `/job-updates`.
