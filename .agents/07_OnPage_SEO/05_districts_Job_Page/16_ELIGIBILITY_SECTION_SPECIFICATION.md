## CONTENT EDITING RULES

**IMPORTANT:** When updating or creating any content based on this specification, follow these human editing guidelines:

### Primary Objective
Transform draft content into natural, original, reader-first writing while preserving factual meaning, important facts, numbers, dates, names, terminology, search intent, primary topic, important keywords, useful supporting information, intended audience, actual purpose of the page, and legitimate claims supported by source material.

### Key Requirements
1. **Remove generic AI-style openings** - Avoid predictable openings like "In today's digital world...", "In this comprehensive guide...", "Let's dive in...". Start with direct, useful information.
2. **Remove repetition** - Check for repeated ideas, keywords, conclusions, explanations, adjectives, sentence structures, headings, and calls to action.
3. **Vary sentence structure** - Mix short, medium, and long sentences. Avoid predictable grammatical patterns.
4. **Improve paragraph flow** - Use transitions only when genuinely helpful. Avoid excessive use of "Furthermore", "Moreover", "Additionally", etc.
5. **Remove formulaic "rule of three" writing** - Avoid unnecessary lists of three similar adjectives unless they provide genuinely different information.
6. **Remove filler** - Delete unnecessary phrases like "It is worth mentioning that", "It should be noted that", "Needless to say", etc.
7. **Use specific language** - Replace vague statements with precise information. Do not add unsupported facts.
8. **Preserve factual accuracy** - Never change dates, statistics, percentages, names, official terminology, URLs, product names, organization names, exam names, job titles, application requirements, eligibility conditions, fees, or deadlines.
9. **Make writing contextual** - Write according to the actual subject (informational, SEO, product, news, educational, job/exam content).
10. **SEO requirements** - Maintain important target keywords naturally. Never stuff keywords or sacrifice readability for SEO.
11. **Heading structure** - Keep logical hierarchy (H1, H2, H3). Use descriptive headings, not generic ones like "Introduction" or "Conclusion".
12. **Lists and tables** - Use bullet points when information is easier to scan as a list. Use tables for structured comparisons.
13. **Remove robotic phrases** - Rewrite templated or generic phrases naturally.
14. **Do not over-humanize** - Do not add random typos, grammar mistakes, excessive slang, fake personal stories, or fake opinions.
15. **Add human-like specificity** - Prefer concrete explanations over generic claims when supported by source material.
16. **Control tone** - Use professional, clear, direct, helpful, natural, confident tone without exaggeration.
17. **Readability** - Make content easy to scan with short paragraphs, clear headings, direct sentences, useful lists, concrete explanations, and logical ordering.
18. **Preserve author's intent** - Do not change the message simply because of personal style preference.
19. **Originality** - Perform genuine rewriting, not superficial synonym replacement. Understand the idea, identify purpose, reorganize wording, rewrite sentence structures, combine repetitive statements, improve clarity, add specificity only when supported, preserve important terminology, and produce genuinely original phrasing.
20. **Final quality check** - Verify content (meaning preserved, no important info removed, no unsupported claims, no changed facts), writing (natural, varied sentences, purposeful paragraphs, no repetition, appropriate formality, no templates), SEO (search intent satisfied, keywords natural, no stuffing, useful headings, crawlable structure), readability (quick understanding, broken long sentences, reasonable paragraph sizes, useful lists), and quality (precise wording, qualified claims, genuinely useful, valuable sections).

### Output Rule
Return ONLY the rewritten content unless explicitly asked for explanation or audit. Do not mention AI detection, humanization, or bypassing detectors. The objective is high-quality human-readable content, not detector manipulation.

## Section Content

### Introduction Paragraph

```
Eligibility requirements for Maharashtra government jobs vary by recruitment notification, department, and position type. Each recruitment notification specifies the educational qualification, age limit, experience requirements, and other eligibility criteria. Candidates must carefully read the official notification to verify they meet the eligibility requirements before applying.
```

---

## Eligibility Categories

### Educational Qualification

```
Educational qualification requirements depend on the position and department. Government jobs in Maharashtra are available for 10th pass, 12th pass, ITI certificate holders, diploma holders, graduates, postgraduates, and candidates with technical or professional qualifications. Higher positions typically require graduate or postgraduate qualifications, while entry-level positions may accept 10th or 12th pass candidates. Technical positions may require specific engineering, medical, or professional degrees.
```

---

### Age Limit

```
Age limits for Maharashtra government jobs vary by recruitment and category. General category candidates typically have age limits ranging from 18 to 38 years depending on the position. Age relaxation is provided for reserved categories including SC, ST, OBC, and other eligible groups. Ex-servicemen, persons with disabilities, and other specified categories may receive additional age relaxation as per government rules. The age calculation date is specified in each recruitment notification.
```

---

### Age Relaxation

```
Age relaxation is available for reserved categories and eligible groups as per Maharashtra government rules. SC and ST candidates typically receive 5 years of age relaxation, OBC candidates receive 3 years, and other eligible categories receive relaxation as specified in the recruitment notification. Ex-servicemen, persons with disabilities, and other categories may receive additional relaxation based on government regulations. Candidates should check the age relaxation applicable to their category in the official notification.
```

---

### Domicile Requirements

```
Some Maharashtra government recruitments may have domicile requirements specifying that candidates must be residents of Maharashtra or specific districts. Domicile requirements vary by recruitment and department. Some positions are open to candidates from across India, while others may require Maharashtra domicile or district-level residency. Candidates should verify domicile requirements in the official notification before applying.
```

---

### Reservation Requirements

```
Maharashtra government jobs follow reservation policies for SC, ST, OBC, and other eligible categories as per state government rules. Reservation percentages and eligible categories are specified in each recruitment notification. Some positions may have unreserved (open) category vacancies. Candidates must provide valid caste certificate and other reservation documents if applying under reserved categories. Reservation rules and eligibility should be verified from the official notification.
```

---

### Experience Requirements

```
Some government positions, particularly technical, administrative, or specialist roles, may require prior work experience. Experience requirements vary by position and are specified in the recruitment notification. Experience may be required in specific fields, government service, or relevant industries. Candidates must provide valid experience certificates and documents if experience is required. Many entry-level positions do not require prior experience.
```

---

### Technical and Professional Qualifications

```
Technical positions in engineering, healthcare, IT, and other fields require specific technical or professional qualifications. These may include engineering degrees, medical degrees, nursing qualifications, IT certifications, or other professional certifications. Technical qualifications must be from recognized institutions and universities. Candidates should verify the specific technical qualifications required for each position from the official notification.
```

---

### Physical Standards

```
Positions in police, forest department, and some other government departments may have physical standards requirements including height, chest measurement, physical fitness, and endurance tests. Physical standards vary by position and gender. Candidates must meet the specified physical standards and pass physical efficiency tests as part of the selection process. Physical requirements are detailed in the recruitment notification.
```

---

### Language Requirements

```
Some Maharashtra government positions may require knowledge of Marathi language, English language, or both. Language requirements vary by position and department. Candidates may need to pass language proficiency tests or provide language qualification certificates. Language requirements are specified in the recruitment notification and should be verified before applying.
```

---

## Important Warning

```
Eligibility requirements vary by recruitment. Candidates should always verify the latest official notification before applying. The information provided here is general guidance and specific eligibility criteria are detailed in each recruitment notification.
```
