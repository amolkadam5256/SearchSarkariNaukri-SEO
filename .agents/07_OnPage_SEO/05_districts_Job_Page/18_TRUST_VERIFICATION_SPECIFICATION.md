## Section Content

### Recruitment Information Changes

```
Recruitment notifications can change, be extended, cancelled, or updated by the recruiting authority. Vacancies may be increased or decreased, application deadlines may be extended, eligibility criteria may be modified, or recruitment may be cancelled entirely. Candidates should verify the latest information through official sources before submitting an application.
```

---

### Official Notification Authority

```
The official recruitment notification issued by the recruiting organization is the final authority for all recruitment details. This includes eligibility criteria, vacancy numbers, application process, selection procedure, important dates, fees, and all other recruitment-related information. Candidates should rely only on the official notification for accurate and up-to-date information.
```

---

### Information Verification

```
Candidates should verify recruitment details against the official notification and official application portal before applying. Check the recruiting organization's official website, government portals, and official recruitment websites for the latest information. Do not rely on unverified sources or outdated information when making application decisions.
```

---

### Application Portal Verification

```
Apply only through the official application portal or method specified by the recruiting organization. Verify that you are on the correct official website before submitting personal information or making payments. Official government recruitment websites typically use .gov.in domains or official organization domains. Be cautious of fraudulent websites or unofficial sources.
```

---

### SearchSarkariNaukri Role

```
SearchSarkariNaukri brings government recruitment information together in one place to help candidates discover relevant job opportunities. The platform organizes recruitment notifications by location, qualification, category, and other parameters to make job search more efficient. The recruiting organization named in each listing is the source of the recruitment notification.
```

---

### Platform Affiliation

```
SearchSarkariNaukri is an information platform and is not affiliated with any government body. The platform aggregates recruitment information from official sources to help candidates find opportunities. Candidates should verify all recruitment details directly with the official recruiting organization before applying.
```

---

### Official Source Links

```
Where available, job listings should provide links to the official notification or official organization website. Candidates should use these links to access the official source and verify recruitment details. Official source links should be to government websites (.gov.in) or official organization websites.
```
