## Core FAQ Questions

### What are government jobs?
**Answer:** Government jobs are employment opportunities recruited by central or state government departments and other public-sector organisations through defined recruitment processes. Eligibility varies by notification.

### Where can I find the latest government jobs?
**Answer:** The Government Jobs page lists active recruitment opportunities and lets candidates discover vacancies by qualification, location, department, exam and application deadline.

### Which government jobs are available for 10th pass candidates?
**Answer:** Depending on current recruitment notifications, 10th pass candidates may find vacancies in different departments and organisations. Eligibility must be checked for each individual recruitment.

### Which government jobs are available for 12th pass candidates?
**Answer:** 12th pass candidates can find recruitment opportunities across different departments and job categories when the notification accepts Class 12 as the required qualification.

### Can graduates apply for government jobs?
**Answer:** Yes. Many government recruitment processes accept graduates, but the required degree, age limit, experience and other conditions vary by recruitment.

### Are there government jobs for freshers?
**Answer:** Some government recruitment is open to candidates without prior work experience. Candidates should check the experience requirement in each official notification.

### How do I apply for a government job?
**Answer:** Open the relevant recruitment details, check eligibility and dates, read the official notification and submit the application through the official portal or method specified by the recruiting organisation.

### Where can I check the official notification?
**Answer:** The individual job page should provide the official notification or official organisation link when available. Candidates should verify recruitment details against that source.

### How can I check the last date for a government job?
**Answer:** The job listing should display the application deadline, but candidates should confirm the current date in the official notification because recruiting organisations can modify deadlines.

### What is the difference between central and state government jobs?
**Answer:** Central government recruitment is conducted by national-level departments and organisations, while state government recruitment is conducted by state departments, boards, corporations and state-level recruitment bodies.

### What is the difference between a government job and a PSU job?
**Answer:** A government department directly employs government staff, while a PSU is a public-sector enterprise with its own recruitment process. The exact employment structure depends on the organisation.

### Are all government jobs available across India?
**Answer:** No. Some vacancies are open to eligible candidates across India, while others are limited by state, district, domicile, language, post or other eligibility conditions.

### How often is this Government Jobs page updated?
**Answer:** The inventory should be updated whenever new recruitment information is added, a deadline changes or a vacancy becomes closed. Display the actual last-updated date generated by the system.

### Can I find Maharashtra government jobs here?
**Answer:** Yes, when active Maharashtra recruitment is available, users can discover Maharashtra vacancies and use state/district filters or the dedicated Maharashtra Government Jobs page.

### How can I find government jobs near me?
**Answer:** Use the location filter to select your state, district or city. For broad district discovery, use the Government Jobs by District resource.

### Which government jobs are available after 10th?
**Answer:** Available opportunities vary over time. Use the 10th Pass Government Jobs filter/page and confirm the qualification requirements for each recruitment.

### Which government jobs are available after 12th?
**Answer:** Available opportunities vary by notification. Use the 12th Pass Government Jobs category and check the exact eligibility of each vacancy.

### What government jobs can graduates apply for?
**Answer:** Graduates may be eligible for recruitment across administration, banking, teaching, technical, research and other categories depending on the qualification and notification.

### How can I find government jobs with upcoming deadlines?
**Answer:** Use the Government Jobs Closing Soon section or filter vacancies by their application deadline.

### Should I verify a government job before applying?
**Answer:** Yes. Read the official recruitment notification and confirm the organisation, eligibility, dates, fees and application method before submitting an application.
