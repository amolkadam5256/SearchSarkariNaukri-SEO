## 3. Strict Editorial & Anti-Stuffing Constraints

- **No Fabricated Search Metrics:** Do NOT make unverified claims such as "This is the #1 trending job in Maharashtra" or publish invented monthly search volumes without verified Google Search Console / Ahrefs data.
- **Geographic Accuracy:** DGFT is a central government body with headquarters in New Delhi and regional offices across India. **Do NOT categorize this as a "Maharashtra State Government Job"** without explicit recruitment circular evidence specifying Maharashtra duty stations.
- **Natural Keyword Density:** Ensure keywords are woven into complete, grammatical sentences. Avoid repetitive bulleted lists repeating `DGFT Recruitment 2026` at the start of every sentence.
- **Multilingual Separation:** Maintain clear visual separation between English and Marathi blocks to allow clean indexing and voice assistant parsing.
