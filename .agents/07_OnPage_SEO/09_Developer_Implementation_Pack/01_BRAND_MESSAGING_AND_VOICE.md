# Brand messaging and voice — for developers and content

## Who we are (use everywhere disclaimers are needed)

SearchSarkariNaukri helps candidates **find and understand** government recruitment notices. We are **not** a government website. We do **not** accept applications or fees. Every form is submitted on the **recruiting authority’s own portal**.

## Tone

- Plain English (and Marathi where the page already uses it). Short sentences. No hype.  
- Write like a careful senior candidate explaining things to a friend — not like a marketing bot.  
- Say “check the official PDF” instead of “guaranteed selection” or “100% authentic”.  
- Use **dates, org names, and links** from the database — never guess.

## Words we use

- Sarkari Naukri, government jobs, recruitment, notification, vacancy, last date, eligibility, admit card, result, answer key, Employment News, Rozgar Samachar.  
- Maharashtra-specific when relevant: MPSC, Police Bharti, Talathi, Zilla Parishad, Mahaswayam.

## Words we avoid

- “Apply here” on our domain (use “Apply on official site”).  
- “Government approved portal” about ourselves.  
- “Updated every 2 minutes” unless engineering confirms that interval.  
- Keyword stuffing (“Sarkari Naukri 2026 Sarkari Job Govt Job…”).

## Semantic SEO habits

- One `<h1>` per page; sections as `<h2>`, sub-points as `<h3>`.  
- Use `<article>`, `<section>`, `<nav aria-label="Breadcrumb">`, `<dl>` for fact blocks where appropriate.  
- Link with descriptive anchors: “MPSC Rajyaseva 2026 recruitment” not “click here”.  
- Facts that change (last date, vacancies) must come from `[DYNAMIC]` fields, not hard-coded in static HTML.

## Competitor parity (MySarkariNaukri)

Match their **coverage and clarity** (state list, answer keys, Employment News, PDF links), not their exact sentences. Our **edge** is verification copy, Maharashtra depth, tools (eligibility, calendar, quiz), and bilingual support.
