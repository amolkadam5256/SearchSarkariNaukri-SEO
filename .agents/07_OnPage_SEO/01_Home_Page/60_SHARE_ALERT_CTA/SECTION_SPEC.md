# Section 60 - Share Alert CTA

## Purpose

End the homepage with a practical call to action for alerts and sharing.

## Recommended UI

- Heading: `Never Miss A Government Job Update`
- Description: `Get alerts for new jobs, closing dates, admit cards, results and exam updates.`
- CTAs: Create Job Alert, Share With Friend, Join Updates, View Latest Jobs.

## SEO Keywords

- government job alerts
- Sarkari Naukri alerts
- latest job notification alerts

## Links

- `/job-alerts`
- `/latest-government-jobs`
- `/admit-cards`
- `/results`

## Rules

No fake urgency. Alert claims must match actual functionality.

## Performance And Accessibility

Use a lightweight final CTA. Do not load third-party widgets by default.
