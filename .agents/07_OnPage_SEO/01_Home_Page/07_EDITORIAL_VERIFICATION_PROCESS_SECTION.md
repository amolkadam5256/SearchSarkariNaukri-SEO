## 📝 Section Specification & Bilingual Copywriting

### Main Section Heading (H2)

```
How We Verify Government Jobs / आम्ही सरकारी नोकऱ्या कशा सत्यापित करतो
```

**Sub-headline / Intro:**
> At SearchSarkariNaukri, every **Sarkari Naukri (सरकारी नोकरी)** notification goes through a structured 7-step editorial verification process before it is published on our platform. We believe candidates deserve **accurate, official, and up-to-date (अचूक आणि अधिकृत)** government recruitment information.

---

### 7-Step Verification Process (H3 for each step)

#### 📋 Step 1: Find Official Notification (अधिकृत जाहिरात शोधणे) (H3)

**Bilingual Copy:**
> Our editorial team actively monitors official government recruitment portals and websites — MPSC (mpsc.gov.in), MahaRecruitment (mahaonline.gov.in), MahaPolice, SSC (ssc.gov.in), RRB (indianrailways.gov.in), IBPS, and Gazette of India — for new recruitment notifications **every single day (दररोज)**.

**Keywords:** `official notification source`, `अधिकृत जाहिरात स्रोत`, `MPSC official notification`

---

#### 📋 Step 2: Identify the Recruiting Organization (भरती संस्था ओळखणे) (H3)

**Bilingual Copy:**
> We identify and confirm the **official recruiting organization (अधिकृत भरती संस्था)** — whether it is MPSC, Railway Recruitment Board (RRB), Staff Selection Commission (SSC), IBPS, a State Government Department, or a District-level body — before proceeding with any data entry.

**Keywords:** `government recruitment eligibility check`, `verified government job information`

---

#### 📋 Step 3: Verify Vacancy Information (जागांची संख्या तपासणे) (H3)

**Bilingual Copy:**
> Total vacancy count, post-wise breakdown, category-wise reservation (SC, ST, OBC, EWS), pay scale (7th CPC or State Pay Matrix), and job location are verified directly from the **official government PDF notification (अधिकृत जाहिरात PDF)** — not from secondary news sources.

**Keywords:** `government job official PDF`, `सरकारी नोकरी अधिकृत PDF`, `verified government job information`

---

#### 📋 Step 4: Verify Eligibility Conditions (पात्रता अटी तपासणे) (H3)

**Bilingual Copy:**
> Educational qualification requirements (10th, 12th, ITI, Diploma, Degree), minimum and maximum age limits, age relaxation rules for reserved categories, domicile conditions, and any special criteria (e.g. Marathi medium, physical fitness) are verified from the official notification before publishing.

**Keywords:** `government recruitment eligibility check`, `सरकारी भरती पात्रता तपासणी`, `10th pass government jobs`

---

#### 📋 Step 5: Verify Application Dates (अर्जाच्या तारखा तपासणे) (H3)

**Bilingual Copy:**
> We verify the exact **application start date**, **last date to apply (अर्जाची शेवटची तारीख)**, **exam date** (if announced), **admit card date**, and **result date** directly from the official notification PDF. All dates displayed on SearchSarkariNaukri are sourced from the original document, not press releases.

**Keywords:** `official application dates verification`, `government jobs last date`, `अर्जाची शेवटची तारीख`

---

#### 📋 Step 6: Link to Official Source (अधिकृत स्रोताशी दुवा जोडणे) (H3)

**Bilingual Copy:**
> Every published job listing includes a direct hyperlink to the **official recruitment advertisement (अधिकृत जाहिरात)** on the recruiting organization's website and to the **official online application portal (ऑनलाईन अर्ज पोर्टल)**. We never redirect candidates through intermediate or affiliate-linked pages.

**Keywords:** `official notification links`, `अधिकृत जाहिरात दुवे`, `sarkari naukri authentic source`

---

#### 📋 Step 7: Update Corrections in Real Time (बदल त्वरित अपडेट करणे) (H3)

**Bilingual Copy:**
> If the recruiting organization issues a corrigendum, date extension, or vacancy revision, our team updates the listing on SearchSarkariNaukri the same day. We display a **"Last Updated" timestamp (शेवटचे अपडेट)** on every listing so candidates always know how current the information is.

**Keywords:** `daily government job updates`, `how government jobs are verified`, `verified government job information`
