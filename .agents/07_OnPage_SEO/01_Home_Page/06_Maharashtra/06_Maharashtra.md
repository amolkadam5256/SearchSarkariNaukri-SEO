## 2. Key Recruitment Sectors for Maharashtra

1. **MPSC (Maharashtra Public Service Commission)**: State Services, Rajyaseva, Combined Group B & C.
2. **Maharashtra Police Bharti**: Police Constable, SRPF, Driver Constable, Bandsman.
3. **Revenue & Land Records**: Talathi, Mandal Adhikari, Nayab Tehsildar.
4. **Rural Development (Zilla Parishad)**: Gram Sevak, Shikshan Sevak, Arogya Sevak, Junior Engineer.
5. **Power & Energy PSUs**: MSEB / MSEDCL (MahaVitaran), MahaTransco, MahaGenco.
6. **Municipal Corporations**: BMC (Mumbai), PMC (Pune), PCMC, NMC (Nagpur), KDMC, VVCMC.
