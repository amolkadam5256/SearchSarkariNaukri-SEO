Design rules:

- Keep the countdown visually dominant.
- Keep links compact and crawlable.
- Use short labels instead of long descriptions.
- Do not add extra visible paragraphs beyond the necessary section content.
- Do not show stale or unverified exam dates.
- Replace the countdown with an updated exam status after the exam date passes.
