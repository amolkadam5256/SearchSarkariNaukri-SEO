# Section 20 - City Jobs

## Purpose

Help users find government jobs in important cities.

## Recommended UI

- Heading: `Government Jobs By City`
- Description: `Explore Sarkari Naukri updates in major cities and nearby regions.`
- Highlight Pune, Mumbai, Nagpur, Nashik, Bangalore, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Jaipur, Lucknow, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad.

## SEO Keywords

- government jobs by city
- Sarkari Naukri in Pune
- government jobs in Mumbai
- latest jobs by city

## Links

Use canonical city landing pages such as `/jobs-in-pune`, `/jobs-in-mumbai`, `/jobs-in-nagpur` only if live and useful.

## Rules

If a city page is thin, noindex it or improve content before adding to sitemap.

## Performance And Accessibility

Use simple grouped links and avoid heavy maps.
