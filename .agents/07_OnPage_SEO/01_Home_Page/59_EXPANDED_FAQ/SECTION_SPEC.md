Add page-specific homepage FAQs that address common Sarkari Naukri discovery and verification questions.

- Heading: `Sarkari Naukri FAQs`
- Add 10-15 concise FAQs.

## SEO Keywords

- Sarkari Naukri FAQ
- government jobs FAQ
- latest government jobs questions

## FAQ Topics

- how to find latest government jobs
- how to verify official notification
- where to check admit cards

- how eligibility is decided

- how Maharashtra jobs are organized

Accordions must be keyboard-accessible. Do not hide all text from crawlers.
