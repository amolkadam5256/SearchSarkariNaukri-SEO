# Section 17 - Women Jobs

Create a respectful discovery section for jobs and resources relevant to women candidates.

- Heading: `Government Jobs For Women`
- Description: `Explore government recruitment updates, eligibility guidance and departments commonly searched by women candidates.`
- Include links for teaching, health, police, banking, clerical and state jobs where pages exist.
