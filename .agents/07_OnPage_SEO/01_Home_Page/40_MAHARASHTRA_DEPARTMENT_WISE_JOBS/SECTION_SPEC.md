# Section 40 - Maharashtra Department-Wise Jobs

## Purpose

Help users browse Maharashtra jobs by state department.

## Recommended UI

- Heading: `Maharashtra Department-Wise Jobs`
- Description: `Explore Maharashtra government vacancies from revenue, police, forest, health, education, rural development, urban development and other departments.`

## SEO Keywords

- Maharashtra department wise jobs
- Maharashtra government department recruitment
- revenue police health education jobs Maharashtra

## Links

Use live department pages for Revenue, Police, Forest, Health, Education, ZP/Rural Development, Urban Development, Agriculture, PWD, Water Resources.

## Rules

Do not create dead department links. If a department page is thin, improve it before indexing.

## Performance And Accessibility

Use grouped chips or cards.
