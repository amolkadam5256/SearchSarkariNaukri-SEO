## 1. Scope & Trust/EEAT Strategy

### Key Trust Pillars
- **Direct Official Sources Only:** We exclusively publish notices sourced from `.gov.in`, `.nic.in`, and official departmental recruitment gazettes.
- **Human Cross-Check Protocol:** Every notification is checked by researchers for eligibility dates, fee deadlines, and authentic links before going live.
- **Anti-Fraud Warning:** Clear disclaimer warning candidates against fraudulent middlemen, fake fees, and phishing recruitment portals.
