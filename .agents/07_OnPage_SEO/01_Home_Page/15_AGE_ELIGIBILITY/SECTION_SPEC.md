# Section 15 - Age Eligibility

Guide users to eligibility tools and age-limit resources without inventing job-specific rules.

## Recommended UI

- Heading: `Check Age And Eligibility`
- Description: `Understand common age limits, qualification rules, category relaxation and document requirements before applying.`
- Include CTA to eligibility checker and guides.
