# Section 13 - Qualification

- Heading: `Government Jobs By Qualification`
- Description: `Find Sarkari Naukri opportunities for 10th pass, 12th pass, ITI, diploma, graduate, engineering and medical qualifications.`
- Use compact cards or chips with active job count where available.
