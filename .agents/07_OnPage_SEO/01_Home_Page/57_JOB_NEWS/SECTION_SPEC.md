# Section 57 - Job News

## Purpose

Surface recruitment news and update articles.

## Recommended UI

- Heading: `Latest Job News`
- Description: `Read recruitment news, notification updates, exam changes and official-source alerts.`

## SEO Keywords

- government job news
- Sarkari Naukri news
- recruitment news India

## Links

- `/job-news`
- `/blog`
- latest news article URLs

## Rules

Separate news from official notification pages. Do not present news as final official instructions.

## Performance And Accessibility

Use 3-4 article cards and lazy-load images.
