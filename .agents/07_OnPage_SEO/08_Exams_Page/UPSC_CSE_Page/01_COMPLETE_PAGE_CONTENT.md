## What is UPSC CSE?

Heading:

```text
What is the UPSC Civil Services Examination?
```

Content:

```text
The UPSC Civil Services Examination is a national-level competitive examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission. It is used for recruitment to civil services and central services, including the Indian Administrative Service, Indian Police Service, Indian Foreign Service and other notified services/posts.

The selection process has three broad stages: Civil Services Preliminary Examination, Civil Services Main written examination and Personality Test/Interview. The Preliminary Examination is a screening stage, while the Main written examination and Personality Test contribute to final merit according to UPSC rules.
```

## FAQ

### What is UPSC CSE?

UPSC CSE is the Civil Services Examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission for recruitment to IAS, IPS, Indian Foreign Service and other notified Central Services.

### Who conducts UPSC Civil Services Examination?

The Union Public Service Commission conducts the Civil Services Examination.

### What qualification is required for UPSC CSE?

A bachelor's degree from a recognized institution is the core qualification, subject to the detailed conditions in the current official UPSC notification.

### What is the UPSC CSE age limit?

The UPSC CSE age limit depends on the current notification, category and cut-off date. Candidates should calculate age using the official cut-off date for that exam year.

### How many attempts are allowed for UPSC CSE?

The number of attempts depends on category and official notification rules. Attempts are also subject to the age limit.

### Is CSAT qualifying in UPSC Prelims?

General Studies Paper II, commonly called CSAT, has a qualifying requirement according to UPSC rules. Candidates should verify the current qualifying rule from the official notification.

### Are Prelims marks included in final merit?

No. Prelims is used as a screening stage. Final merit is based on Main merit papers and Personality Test according to UPSC rules.

### How many papers are there in UPSC Mains?

UPSC Mains includes qualifying language papers and merit papers including Essay, General Studies papers and Optional subject papers. Check the current official scheme for exact paper and marks details.

### What is an optional subject in UPSC CSE?

The optional subject is one subject selected by the candidate for two Main examination papers, subject to the current UPSC scheme.

### What services are available through UPSC CSE?

UPSC CSE can lead to IAS, IPS, Indian Foreign Service and other notified Central Services. The final service list should be checked from the current official notification.

### Where can I download previous UPSC question papers?

Previous UPSC question papers are available from the official UPSC previous-question-papers section.

### Where should I verify official UPSC dates and rules?

Verify dates, eligibility, attempts, syllabus, fees and application rules from the official UPSC website and current official notification.
