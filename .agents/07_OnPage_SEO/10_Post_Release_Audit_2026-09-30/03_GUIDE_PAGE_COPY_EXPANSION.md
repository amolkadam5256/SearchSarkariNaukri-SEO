## H2 — One-time registration (OTR) on major portals

Many central recruitments expect you to register once on the commission portal, then apply advertisement-by-advertisement.

| Portal | Typical use | Official entry |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| UPSC | CSE, CDS, NDA, CAPF, etc. | https://upsconline.nic.in |
| SSC | CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD, CPO | https://ssc.nic.in |
| RRB | NTPC, Group D, ALP (zone in notification) | https://www.rrcb.gov.in |
| IBPS | PO, Clerk, RRB banks | https://www.ibps.in |

Keep your registration number, password, and registered mobile/email safe. If you change phone numbers, update the profile **before** the last date — OTP recovery during the final hour fails often.

## H2 — Application fee: what to check

- Fee amount and category-wise exemption (SC/ST/PwBD/female/ex-servicemen — **only if the notice says so**).
- Payment mode: UPI, net banking, challan, or fee exempt with certificate upload.
- Fee is **non-refundable** in most notifications even if you do not appear for the exam.
- Never pay any “agent” on SearchSarkariNaukri or social media — fees go only to the account named in the PDF.

## H2 — Common mistakes that cause rejection

- Wrong date of birth or name spelling vs 10th certificate.
- Uploading photo/signature over size limits or wrong background.
- Selecting wrong category without valid certificate.
- Applying for a post whose domicile or language rule you do not meet.
- Missing the fee step — “saved draft” is not a submitted application until confirmation is generated.

Use the [eligibility checker](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/eligibility-checker) as a first filter, then read the notification table again.

## H2 — Tracking admit card, exam, and result

After you apply, add these to your calendar:

1. **Admit card release** — download only from the commission site; check [admit cards](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/admit-cards) for aggregated updates.
2. **Exam city / shift** — verify in admit card; reach early with ID and photo.
3. **Answer key** — provisional then final; objections only in the window the notice gives (official site, not third-party PDFs).
4. **Result / merit list** — [results](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/results); keep application number and roll number safe.

See also the [exam calendar](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/exam-calendar) for overlapping exam dates if you hold multiple applications.

## Additional FAQ (add to page + FAQPage schema if published)

### What if I forget my application password on the last day?

Use the portal’s “forgot password” immediately. Commission helpdesks are overloaded on closing day — reset early.

### Can I edit my application after submit?

Only if the notification allows correction window or payment correction. Otherwise you may need to re-apply if the portal permits multiple applications.

### Is Employment News mandatory reading?

It is an official source for many central advertisements. When our [Employment News hub](https://www.searchsarkarinaukri.com/employment-news) is live, use it alongside official PDFs — until then, use https://www.employmentnews.gov.in directly.
