# Keyword Research Strategy

## Purpose
- Core methodology for effective keyword research
- Aligns with SEO best practices and business goals
- Establishes consistent process across projects

## Components
1. [seed_keywords]: Source of initial keyword foundation
2. [search_intent]: Analysis of user search behavior
3. [keyword_clusters]: Organized semantic groups
4. [competitor_research]: Benchmark against competitors
5. [long_tail]: Targeting less competitive terms
6. [local_seo]: Location-specific keyword optimization
7. [tooling]: Recommended research platforms
8. [mapping]: Strategic keyword-content alignment
9. [tracking]: Performance measurement framework

## Process Flow
1. Seed keywords -> 2. Intent mapping -> 3. Cluster formation -> 4. Competitor comparison -> 5. Long tail opportunities -> 6. Local optimization -> 7. Tool implementation -> 8. Content mapping -> 9. Performance tracking

## Success Metrics
- 15% increase in organic traffic
- 20% improvement in keyword difficulty
- 90% coverage of content clusters
- 25% higher conversion rate for target keywords
