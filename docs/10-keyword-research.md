# 10 — Keyword Strategy, Intent Mapping & Seasonal Search Calendar

## 10.1 3-Step Keyword Research Framework

1. **Seed Discovery:** Identify root entity keywords across Core Brand (`sarkari naukri`), State (`UP govt jobs`), Department (`railway bharti`), and Qualification (`10th pass jobs`).
2. **Expansion & Intent Qualification:** Expand seeds using Google Keyword Planner, GSC Query data, Autocomplete, and AnswerThePublic; qualify intent (Transactional vs Informational vs Commercial vs Navigational).
3. **SERP Feature Analysis:** Map whether queries trigger AI Overviews, Google Jobs Carousels, PAA accordions, or Video carousels.
