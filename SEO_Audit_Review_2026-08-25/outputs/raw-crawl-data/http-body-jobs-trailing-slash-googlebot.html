Frequently Asked Questions

How often are government jobs updated on SearchSarkariNaukri?
Job listings refresh hourly from our automated scrapers (MahaBharti daily 07:30 IST, MHGovtJobs 06:30 IST, Employment News weekly Monday 09:00 IST, plus official-source scrapers for UPSC, SSC, IBPS, MPSC). Manual additions from official notifications can land within minutes.
Are the application links official?
Yes. Every "Apply" link on a job page points at the recruiting authority's own portal — typically a .gov.in, .nic.in or recognised state-portal domain. We never link to aggregator sites or third-party form hosts.
Is the service free?
Yes. Browsing job listings, the eligibility checker, age calculator and the daily WhatsApp/Telegram alert channel are all free. SearchSarkariNaukri does not collect any government application fee or candidate payment — see our Refund / No-Payment Policy.
How do I get instant alerts for new government jobs?
Join our WhatsApp Community and Telegram Channel from the Job Updates page. You'll get daily-curated Sarkari Naukri updates and can opt out anytime by replying STOP.
Which categories of government jobs are covered?
Central Government, State Government (Maharashtra focus), Banking & Finance, Railway, Defence, Medical & Health, Education & Research, PSU & Corporations, Police Bharti, and Teacher recruitment (TET / CTET).
How do I check if I am eligible for a particular post?
Use the Eligibility Checker tool — enter your qualification, age and category to see all matching jobs. The Age Calculator additionally shows category-wise relaxation (SC, ST, OBC, EWS, PwBD, Ex-Serviceman).
